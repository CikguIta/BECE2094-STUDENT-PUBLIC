Part A — Lecture Notes

2.1 International Policies/Acts
2.1 国际政策／法令
2.1 Guójì zhèngcè / fǎlìng

A. What are international policies?

International policies and agreements help countries protect the rights of children.
国际政策和协议帮助各国保护儿童的权利。
Guójì zhèngcè hé xiéyì bāngzhù gèguó bǎohù értóng de quánlì.
They encourage countries to provide education, healthcare, safety and equal opportunities.
它们鼓励各国提供教育、医疗、安全保障和平等机会。
Tāmen gǔlì gèguó tígōng jiàoyù, yīliáo, ānquán bǎozhàng hé píngděng jīhuì.
Children with disabilities have the same human rights as other children.
残障儿童与其他儿童享有相同的人权。
Cánzhàng értóng yǔ qítā értóng xiǎngyǒu xiāngtóng de rénquán.
International conventions are agreements between countries. They are not automatically the same as national laws.
国际公约是国家之间的协议。它们不自动等同于国内法律。
Guójì gōngyuē shì guójiā zhījiān de xiéyì. Tāmen bù zìdòng děngtóng yú guónèi fǎlǜ.

B. The importance of international agreements

1. Equal opportunities
1. 平等机会
1. Píngděng jīhuì
Every child should have opportunities to learn and participate.
每个儿童都应该有学习和参与的机会。
Měi gè értóng dōu yīnggāi yǒu xuéxí hé cānyù de jīhuì.
Preschool example: A child who uses a wheelchair joins an art activity at an accessible table.
幼儿园例子： 一名使用轮椅的儿童在方便使用的桌子旁参加美术活动。
Yòu'éryuán lìzi: Yì míng shǐyòng lúnyǐ de értóng zài fāngbiàn shǐyòng de zhuōzi páng cānjiā měishù huódòng.
2. Protection from discrimination
2. 保护儿童免受歧视
2. Bǎohù értóng miǎn shòu qíshì
A child should not be treated unfairly because of a disability.
儿童不应该因为残障而受到不公平的对待。
Értóng bù yīnggāi yīnwèi cánzhàng ér shòudào bù gōngpíng de duìdài.
Preschool example: A child with hearing loss is included in a music-and-movement activity.
幼儿园例子： 一名有听力损失的儿童也参与音乐律动活动。
Yòu'éryuán lìzi: Yì míng yǒu tīnglì sǔnshī de értóng yě cānyù yīnyuè lǜdòng huódòng.
The teacher uses visual cues and demonstrations to support participation.
教师使用视觉提示和示范来帮助儿童参与。
Jiàoshī shǐyòng shìjué tíshì hé shìfàn lái bāngzhù értóng cānyù.
3. Inclusive education
3. 融合教育
3. Rónghé jiàoyù
Inclusive education means children learn together with appropriate support.
融合教育是指儿童在获得适当支持的情况下共同学习。
Rónghé jiàoyù shì zhǐ értóng zài huòdé shìdàng zhīchí de qíngkuàng xià gòngtóng xuéxí.
Preschool example: A teacher provides picture instructions for a child who has difficulty understanding spoken directions.
幼儿园例子： 教师为难以理解口头指示的儿童提供图片指示。
Yòu'éryuán lìzi: Jiàoshī wèi nányǐ lǐjiě kǒutóu zhǐshì de értóng tígōng túpiàn zhǐshì.

C. UNESCO and inclusive education

UNESCO is an international organisation that supports education for all.
联合国教科文组织是一个支持全民教育的国际组织。
Liánhéguó Jiàokēwén Zǔzhī shì yí gè zhīchí quánmín jiàoyù de guójì zǔzhī.
UNESCO promotes education systems that include learners with different needs.
联合国教科文组织推动能够接纳不同需要学习者的教育制度。
Liánhéguó Jiàokēwén Zǔzhī tuīdòng nénggòu jiēnà bùtóng xūyào xuéxízhě de jiàoyù zhìdù.
An important international document is the Salamanca Statement of 1994, which promotes inclusive education.
一份重要的国际文件是1994年的《萨拉曼卡宣言》，它提倡融合教育。
Yí fèn zhòngyào de guójì wénjiàn shì yī jiǔ jiǔ sì nián de “Sàlāmànkǎ Xuānyán”, tā tíchàng rónghé jiàoyù.
The Salamanca Statement is a policy statement, not a Malaysian Act of Parliament.
《萨拉曼卡宣言》是一份政策宣言，而不是马来西亚国会法令。
“Sàlāmànkǎ Xuānyán” shì yí fèn zhèngcè xuānyán, ér bú shì Mǎláixīyà Guóhuì fǎlìng.

2.2 National Policies/Acts
2.2 国家政策／法令
2.2 Guójiā zhèngcè / fǎlìng

A. Understanding national laws and international conventions

National laws are laws made or adopted through a country's legal system.
国内法律是通过一个国家的法律制度制定或采纳的法律。
Guónèi fǎlǜ shì tōngguò yí gè guójiā de fǎlǜ zhìdù zhìdìng huò cǎinà de fǎlǜ.
In Malaysia, laws such as the Child Act 2001 and Persons with Disabilities Act 2008 provide legal frameworks relevant to children with special needs.
在马来西亚，《2001年儿童法令》和《2008年残疾人法令》等法律提供与特殊需要儿童有关的法律框架。
Zài Mǎláixīyà, “Èr Líng Líng Yī Nián Értóng Fǎlìng” hé “Èr Líng Líng Bā Nián Cánjírén Fǎlìng” děng fǎlǜ tígōng yǔ tèshū xūyào értóng yǒuguān de fǎlǜ kuàngjià.
The CRC and CRPD are international conventions. Malaysia is a party to both, but they should not be described as Malaysian Acts.
《儿童权利公约》和《残疾人权利公约》是国际公约。马来西亚是这两项公约的缔约国，但它们不应被称为马来西亚法令。
“Értóng Quánlì Gōngyuē” hé “Cánjírén Quánlì Gōngyuē” shì guójì gōngyuē. Mǎláixīyà shì zhè liǎng xiàng gōngyuē de dìyuēguó, dàn tāmen bù yīng bèi chēngwéi Mǎláixīyà fǎlìng.

B. Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC)

The CRC is an international agreement adopted by the United Nations in 1989.
《儿童权利公约》是联合国于1989年通过的一项国际协议。
“Értóng Quánlì Gōngyuē” shì Liánhéguó yú yī jiǔ bā jiǔ nián tōngguò de yí xiàng guójì xiéyì.
It recognises the rights of children to protection, development, education and participation.
它承认儿童享有保护、发展、教育和参与的权利。
Tā chéngrèn értóng xiǎngyǒu bǎohù, fāzhǎn, jiàoyù hé cānyù de quánlì.
Article 23 specifically addresses the rights and needs of children with disabilities.
第23条特别涉及残障儿童的权利和需要。
Dì èrshísān tiáo tèbié shèjí cánzhàng értóng de quánlì hé xūyào.
Children with disabilities should be supported to live with dignity and participate in society.
残障儿童应该获得支持，有尊严地生活并参与社会。
Cánzhàng értóng yīnggāi huòdé zhīchí, yǒu zūnyán de shēnghuó bìng cānyù shèhuì.
Preschool example
幼儿园例子
Yòu'éryuán lìzi
A child who has difficulty speaking is encouraged to communicate using pictures or gestures.
一名说话有困难的儿童受到鼓励，可以使用图片或手势沟通。
Yì míng shuōhuà yǒu kùnnan de értóng shòudào gǔlì, kěyǐ shǐyòng túpiàn huò shǒushì gōutōng.
The teacher listens to the child's choices instead of ignoring them.
教师尊重并聆听儿童的选择，而不是忽视他们。
Jiàoshī zūnzhòng bìng língtīng értóng de xuǎnzé, ér bú shì hūshì tāmen.

C. Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD)

The CRPD is an international agreement adopted by the United Nations in 2006.
《残疾人权利公约》是联合国于2006年通过的一项国际协议。
“Cánjírén Quánlì Gōngyuē” shì Liánhéguó yú èr líng líng liù nián tōngguò de yí xiàng guójì xiéyì.
It promotes equal rights, accessibility, participation and inclusion for persons with disabilities.
它促进残障人士的平等权利、无障碍环境、参与和融合。
Tā cùjìn cánzhàng rénshì de píngděng quánlì, wú zhàng'ài huánjìng, cānyù hé rónghé.
Article 7 focuses on children with disabilities.
第7条关注残障儿童。
Dì qī tiáo guānzhù cánzhàng értóng.
Article 24 addresses the right to education, including inclusive education.
第24条涉及教育权，包括融合教育。
Dì èrshísì tiáo shèjí jiàoyùquán, bāokuò rónghé jiàoyù.
Reasonable accommodation means making necessary and appropriate changes to help a person exercise their rights, without imposing a disproportionate or undue burden.
合理便利是指在不造成不相称或过度负担的情况下，作出必要且适当的调整，帮助个人行使其权利。
Hélǐ biànlì shì zhǐ zài bù zàochéng bù xiāngchèn huò guòdù fùdān de qíngkuàng xià, zuòchū bìyào qiě shìdàng de tiáozhěng, bāngzhù gèrén xíngshǐ qí quánlì.
Preschool example
幼儿园例子
Yòu'éryuán lìzi
A teacher gives a child extra time to complete a matching activity.
教师给予一名儿童额外时间完成配对活动。
Jiàoshī jǐyǔ yì míng értóng éwài shíjiān wánchéng pèiduì huódòng.
The child works towards the same learning goal with suitable support.
这名儿童在适当的支持下，朝着相同的学习目标努力。
Zhè míng értóng zài shìdàng de zhīchí xià, cháozhe xiāngtóng de xuéxí mùbiāo nǔlì.

D. Comparing CRC and CRPD

The CRC protects children's rights broadly. The CRPD gives specific attention to disability rights and accessibility.
《儿童权利公约》广泛保护儿童权利。《残疾人权利公约》特别关注残障权利和无障碍环境。
“Értóng Quánlì Gōngyuē” guǎngfàn bǎohù értóng quánlì. “Cánjírén Quánlì Gōngyuē” tèbié guānzhù cánzhàng quánlì hé wú zhàng'ài huánjìng.

2.3 Issues – Concept of Least Restrictive Environments
2.3 相关议题——最少限制环境的概念
2.3 Xiāngguān yìtí — Zuì shǎo xiànzhì huánjìng de gàiniàn

A. What is a least restrictive environment (LRE)?

A least restrictive environment is an educational setting where a child with a disability can learn and participate with peers without disabilities as much as appropriate, with suitable support.
最少限制环境是一种教育环境，让残障儿童在获得适当支持的情况下，尽可能与非残障同伴一起学习和参与活动。
Zuì shǎo xiànzhì huánjìng shì yì zhǒng jiàoyù huánjìng, ràng cánzhàng értóng zài huòdé shìdàng zhīchí de qíngkuàng xià, jǐn kěnéng yǔ fēi cánzhàng tóngbàn yìqǐ xuéxí hé cānyù huódòng.
The goal is meaningful participation, not simply placing every child in the same classroom.
目标是让儿童有意义地参与，而不只是把所有儿童安排在同一间教室。
Mùbiāo shì ràng értóng yǒu yìyì de cānyù, ér bù zhǐshì bǎ suǒyǒu értóng ānpái zài tóng yì jiān jiàoshì.
The term LRE is strongly associated with United States special education law. In Malaysia, we can use the concept to discuss inclusive placement and appropriate support, but should not present it as the wording of a Malaysian law.
“最少限制环境”这一术语与美国特殊教育法律密切相关。在马来西亚，我们可以用这个概念讨论融合安置和适当支持，但不应将它说成马来西亚法律的原文。
“Zuì shǎo xiànzhì huánjìng” zhè yī shùyǔ yǔ Měiguó tèshū jiàoyù fǎlǜ mìqiè xiāngguān. Zài Mǎláixīyà, wǒmen kěyǐ yòng zhège gàiniàn tǎolùn rónghé ānzhì hé shìdàng zhīchí, dàn bù yīng jiāng tā shuō chéng Mǎláixīyà fǎlǜ de yuánwén.

B. Four important principles

1. Participation
1. 参与
1. Cānyù
Children should have opportunities to join learning and play.
儿童应该有机会参与学习和游戏。
Értóng yīnggāi yǒu jīhuì cānyù xuéxí hé yóuxì.
2. Appropriate support
2. 适当支持
2. Shìdàng zhīchí
Teachers should provide support based on each child's needs.
教师应该根据每个儿童的需要提供支持。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi gēnjù měi gè értóng de xūyào tígōng zhīchí.
3. Individual differences
3. 个别差异
3. Gèbié chāyì
Children may need different materials, instructions or learning arrangements.
儿童可能需要不同的材料、指示或学习安排。
Értóng kěnéng xūyào bùtóng de cáiliào, zhǐshì huò xuéxí ānpái.
4. Review and adjustment
4. 检讨与调整
4. Jiǎntǎo yǔ tiáozhěng
Teachers should observe whether the learning arrangement is helping the child.
教师应该观察学习安排是否真正帮助儿童。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi guānchá xuéxí ānpái shìfǒu zhēnzhèng bāngzhù értóng.

C. Preschool examples of less restrictive practices

Example 1: Storytelling
例子一：讲故事
Lìzi yī: Jiǎng gùshi
A child with hearing loss sits where the teacher's face and pictures are clearly visible.
一名有听力损失的儿童坐在能够清楚看到教师脸部和图片的位置。
Yì míng yǒu tīnglì sǔnshī de értóng zuò zài nénggòu qīngchǔ kàndào jiàoshī liǎnbù hé túpiàn de wèizhì.
Example 2: Outdoor play
例子二：户外游戏
Lìzi èr: Hùwài yóuxì
A teacher adjusts a ball game so a child with limited mobility can participate.
教师调整球类游戏，让行动不便的儿童也能参与。
Jiàoshī tiáozhěng qiúlèi yóuxì, ràng xíngdòng bùbiàn de értóng yě néng cānyù.

D. Child Act 2001 (Malaysia)

The Child Act 2001 is an important Malaysian law concerning the care, protection and rehabilitation of children.
《2001年儿童法令》是马来西亚一项有关儿童照顾、保护和康复的重要法律。
“Èr Líng Líng Yī Nián Értóng Fǎlìng” shì Mǎláixīyà yí xiàng yǒuguān értóng zhàogù, bǎohù hé kāngfù de zhòngyào fǎlǜ.
It provides a legal framework for responding when children need care or protection.
它为需要照顾或保护的儿童提供法律应对框架。
Tā wèi xūyào zhàogù huò bǎohù de értóng tígōng fǎlǜ yìngduì kuàngjià.
It is not a law that specifically establishes the LRE principle.
它并不是一项专门确立最少限制环境原则的法律。
Tā bìng bú shì yí xiàng zhuānmén quèlì zuì shǎo xiànzhì huánjìng yuánzé de fǎlǜ.
Preschool example
幼儿园例子
Yòu'éryuán lìzi
A teacher notices repeated signs that a child may be experiencing neglect.
教师注意到一名儿童反复出现可能受到忽视的迹象。
Jiàoshī zhùyì dào yì míng értóng fǎnfù chūxiàn kěnéng shòudào hūshì de jìxiàng.
The teacher records factual observations and follows the centre's safeguarding and reporting procedures.
教师记录客观观察结果，并遵循中心的儿童保护及报告程序。
Jiàoshī jìlù kèguān guānchá jiéguǒ, bìng zūnxún zhōngxīn de értóng bǎohù jí bàogào chéngxù.

E. Persons with Disabilities Act 2008 (Malaysia)

The Persons with Disabilities Act 2008 is a Malaysian law that addresses the rights and interests of persons with disabilities.
《2008年残疾人法令》是马来西亚一项涉及残障人士权利和利益的法律。
“Èr Líng Líng Bā Nián Cánjírén Fǎlìng” shì Mǎláixīyà yí xiàng shèjí cánzhàng rénshì quánlì hé lìyì de fǎlǜ.
It includes provisions relating to access to education and other areas of participation.
它包含与教育机会及其他参与领域有关的条文。
Tā bāohán yǔ jiàoyù jīhuì jí qítā cānyù lǐngyù yǒuguān de tiáowén.
The Act recognises the importance of access and equal opportunities, although legal protections and enforcement mechanisms have limitations.
