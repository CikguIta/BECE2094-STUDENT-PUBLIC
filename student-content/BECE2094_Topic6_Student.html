6.1 Educational Considerations
6.1 教育方面的考量
6.1 Jiàoyù fāngmiàn de kǎoliáng
A. What is ADHD?
A. 什么是 ADHD？
A. Shénme shì ADHD?
ADHD is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects attention, activity level and impulse control.
注意缺陷多动障碍是一种神经发育状况，会影响注意力、活动程度和冲动控制。
Zhùyì quēxiàn duōdòng zhàng'ài shì yì zhǒng shénjīng fāyù zhuàngkuàng, huì yǐngxiǎng zhùyìlì, huódòng chéngdù hé chōngdòng kòngzhì.
Children with ADHD may find it difficult to stay focused, remain still when expected or wait before acting.
有ADHD的儿童可能难以保持专注、在需要时安静坐着，或在行动前等待。
Yǒu ADHD de értóng kěnéng nányǐ bǎochí zhuānzhù, zài xūyào shí ānjìng zuòzhe, huò zài xíngdòng qián děngdài.
ADHD is not simply caused by poor parenting, laziness or a lack of discipline.
ADHD并不只是由不良教养、懒惰或缺乏纪律造成的。
ADHD bìng bù zhǐ shì yóu bùliáng jiàoyǎng, lǎnduò huò quēfá jìlǜ zàochéng de.
Important: Not every active or easily distracted child has ADHD.
重要： 并不是每个活跃或容易分心的儿童都有ADHD。
Zhòngyào: Bìng bú shì měi gè huóyuè huò róngyì fēnxīn de értóng dōu yǒu ADHD.
Young children naturally differ in their attention spans, activity levels and ability to control impulses.
幼儿的注意力持续时间、活动程度和控制冲动的能力本来就有差异。
Yòu'ér de zhùyìlì chíxù shíjiān, huódòng chéngdù hé kòngzhì chōngdòng de nénglì běnlái jiù yǒu chāyì.

3. Combined presentation
3. 混合型表现
Sān. Hùnhé xíng biǎoxiàn
A child shows significant difficulties involving both inattention and hyperactivity-impulsivity.
儿童在注意力不集中以及多动和冲动方面都表现出明显困难。
Értóng zài zhùyìlì bù jízhōng yǐjí duōdòng hé chōngdòng fāngmiàn dōu biǎoxiàn chū míngxiǎn kùnnan.
These presentations describe patterns of symptoms, not fixed personality types.
这些表现类型描述的是症状模式，而不是固定的人格类型。
Zhèxiē biǎoxiàn lèixíng miáoshù de shì zhèngzhuàng móshì, ér bú shì gùdìng de réngé lèixíng.
C. Educational needs of children with ADHD
C. ADHD 儿童的教育需要
C. ADHD értóng de jiàoyù xūyào
1. Clear and short instructions
1. 清楚而简短的指示
Yī. Qīngchǔ ér jiǎnduǎn de zhǐshì
Give one or two instructions at a time instead of a long list.
每次给予一至两项指示，而不是一长串指示。
Měi cì jǐyǔ yī zhì liǎng xiàng zhǐshì, ér bú shì yì cháng chuàn zhǐshì.
2. Predictable routines
2. 可预测的日常程序
Èr. Kě yùcè de rìcháng chéngxù
Use consistent routines and visual schedules to help children know what comes next.
使用一致的日常程序和视觉时间表，帮助儿童知道接下来要做什么。
Shǐyòng yízhì de rìcháng chéngxù hé shìjué shíjiānbiǎo, bāngzhù értóng zhīdào jiēxiàlái yào zuò shénme.
3. Opportunities for movement
3. 提供活动身体的机会
Sān. Tígōng huódòng shēntǐǐ de jīhuì
Provide appropriate movement breaks and active learning activities.
提供适当的活动休息时间和动态学习活动。
Tígōng shìdàng de huódòng xiūxi shíjiān hé dòngtài xuéxí huódòng.
4. Positive behaviour support
4. 积极行为支持
Sì. Jījí xíngwéi zhīchí
Recognise desirable behaviour and provide immediate, specific encouragement.
认可适当的行为，并立即给予具体的鼓励。
Rènkě shìdàng de xíngwéi, bìng lìjí jǐyǔ jùtǐ de gǔlì.
5. Support for self-regulation
5. 自我调节支持
Wǔ. Zìwǒ tiáojié zhīchí
Teach children how to recognise feelings, ask for help and use simple calming strategies.
教导儿童认识情绪、寻求帮助和使用简单的平静策略。
Jiàodǎo értóng rènshi qíngxù, xúnqiú bāngzhù hé shǐyòng jiǎndān de píngjìng cèlüè.
6. Respectful inclusion
6. 尊重与融合
Liù. Zūnzhòng yǔ rónghé
Children should have opportunities to learn, play and develop friendships with their peers.
儿童应该有机会与同伴一起学习、游戏和建立友谊。
Értóng yīnggāi yǒu jīhuì yǔ tóngbàn yìqǐ xuéxí, yóuxì hé jiànlì yǒuyì.
D. What teachers should observe
D. 教师应该观察什么
D. Jiàoshī yīnggāi guānchá shénme
Teachers should record what happens before, during and after a behaviour.
教师应该记录行为发生之前、期间和之后的情况。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi jìlù xíngwéi fāshēng zhīqián, qījiān hé zhīhòu de qíngkuàng.
For example, record how often a child leaves a seat during a five-minute group activity.
例如，记录儿童在五分钟小组活动中离开座位的次数。
Lìrú, jìlù értóng zài wǔ fēnzhōng xiǎozǔ huódòng zhōng líkāi zuòwèi de cìshù.
The teacher should also consider sleep, stress, language understanding, sensory needs and the suitability of the activity.
教师也应该考虑睡眠、压力、语言理解、感官需要以及活动是否合适。
Jiàoshī yě yīnggāi kǎolǜ shuìmián, yālì, yǔyán lǐjiě, gǎnguān xūyào yǐjí huódòng shìfǒu héshì.
Teachers observe and support; qualified healthcare professionals assess and diagnose ADHD.
教师负责观察和支持；ADHD的评估与诊断由合格的医疗专业人员进行。
Jiàoshī fùzé guānchá hé zhīchí; ADHD de pínggū yǔ zhěnduàn yóu hégé de yīliáo zhuānyè rényuán jìnxíng.

6.2 Early Intervention
6.2 早期干预
6.2 Zǎoqī gānyù
A. What is early intervention for ADHD?
A. 什么是 ADHD 的早期干预？
A. Shénme shì ADHD de zǎoqī gānyù?
Early intervention provides support when a young child shows persistent difficulties with attention, activity regulation or impulse control.
当幼儿持续出现注意力、活动调节或冲动控制困难时，早期干预可以提供支持。
Dāng yòu'ér chíxù chūxiàn zhùyìlì, huódòng tiáojié huò chōngdòng kòngzhì kùnnan shí, zǎoqī gānyù kěyǐ tígōng zhīchí.
The purpose is to improve participation, relationships, learning and everyday functioning.
其目的是改善参与、同伴关系、学习和日常生活能力。
Qí mùdì shì gǎishàn cānyù, tóngbàn guānxì, xuéxí hé rìcháng shēnghuó nénglì.
Support should begin when a need is identified, even if the child has not received a formal diagnosis.
一旦发现需要，就应该开始提供支持，即使儿童尚未获得正式诊断。
Yídàn fāxiàn xūyào, jiù yīnggāi kāishǐ tígōng zhīchí, jíshǐ értóng shàngwèi huòdé zhèngshì zhěnduàn.
B. Five early intervention approaches
B. 五种早期干预方法
B. Wǔ zhǒng zǎoqī gānyù fāngfǎ
1. Parent and caregiver behaviour training
1. 家长与照顾者行为管理培训
Yī. Jiāzhǎng yǔ zhàogùzhě xíngwéi guǎnlǐ péixùn
Parents and caregivers can learn to establish predictable routines, give clear instructions and respond consistently to behaviour.
家长和照顾者可以学习建立可预测的日常程序、给予清楚指示，并以一致的方式回应行为。
Jiāzhǎng hé zhàogùzhě kěyǐ xuéxí jiànlì kě yùcè de rìcháng chéngxù, jǐyǔ qīngchǔ zhǐshì, bìng yǐ yízhì de fāngshì huíyìng xíngwéi.
Evidence-based parent training in behaviour management is an important recommended approach for young children with ADHD.
有研究证据支持的家长行为管理培训，是帮助有ADHD幼儿的重要推荐方法。
Yǒu yánjiū zhèngjù zhīchí de jiāzhǎng xíngwéi guǎnlǐ péixùn, shì bāngzhù yǒu ADHD yòu'ér de zhòngyào tuījiàn fāngfǎ.
2. Classroom behaviour support
2. 课堂行为支持
Èr. Kètáng xíngwéi zhīchí
Teachers can use clear expectations, visual reminders and frequent positive feedback.
教师可以使用清楚的行为期望、视觉提醒和经常性的积极反馈。
Jiàoshī kěyǐ shǐyòng qīngchǔ de xíngwéi qīwàng, shìjué tíxǐng hé jīngchángxìng de jījí fǎnkuì.
3. Teach self-regulation skills
3. 教授自我调节技能
Sān. Jiàoshòu zìwǒ tiáojié jìnéng
Help children practise stopping, listening, waiting, identifying emotions and asking for assistance.
帮助儿童练习停下来、聆听、等待、识别情绪和寻求协助。
Bāngzhù értóng liànxí tíng xiàlái, língtīng, děngdài, shíbié qíngxù hé xúnqiú xiézhù.
4. Use developmentally appropriate activities
4. 使用符合发展阶段的活动
Sì. Shǐyòng fúhé fāzhǎn jiēduàn de huódòng
Use short, engaging, play-based activities with appropriate opportunities for movement.
使用简短、有吸引力、以游戏为基础的活动，并提供适当的身体活动机会。
Shǐyòng jiǎnduǎn, yǒu xīyǐnlì, yǐ yóuxì wéi jīchǔ de huódòng, bìng tígōng shìdàng de shēntǐ huódòng jīhuì.
5. Coordinate support across home and school
5. 协调家庭与学校的支持
Wǔ. Xiétiáo jiātíng yǔ xuéxiào de zhīchí
Teachers and families can agree on a small number of meaningful goals and share progress respectfully.
教师与家庭可以共同决定少量有意义的目标，并以尊重的方式分享进展。
Jiàoshī yǔ jiātíng kěyǐ gòngtóng juédìng shǎoliàng yǒu yìyì de mùbiāo, bìng yǐ zūnzhòng de fāngshì fēnxiǎng jìnzhǎn.
C. A simple early intervention process
C. 简单的早期干预过程
C. Jiǎndān de zǎoqī gānyù guòchéng
Observe and record the child's behaviour.
观察并记录儿童的行为。
Guānchá bìng jìlù értóng de xíngwéi.
Identify one meaningful target behaviour.
识别一项有意义的目标行为。
Shíbié yí xiàng yǒu yìyì de mùbiāo xíngwéi.
Adjust the activity and environment.
调整活动和环境。
Tiáozhěng huódòng hé huánjìng.
Teach and reinforce the desired behaviour.
教导并强化期望的行为。
Jiàodǎo bìng qiánghuà qīwàng de xíngwéi.
Monitor progress and work with the family.
追踪进展并与家庭合作。
Zhuīzōng jìnzhǎn bìng yǔ jiātíng hézuò.
D. Example of a measurable goal
D. 可衡量目标的示例
D. Kě héngliáng mùbiāo de shìlì
Situation: A five-year-old child finds it difficult to wait during small-group games.
情境： 一名五岁儿童在小组游戏中难以等待。
Qíngjìng: Yì míng wǔ suì értóng zài xiǎozǔ yóuxì zhōng nányǐ děngdài.
Goal: Within four weeks, the child will wait for a turn for 30 seconds, using a visual cue when needed, in four out of five observed opportunities.
目标： 在四周内，儿童将在需要时使用视觉提示，并在五次观察机会中至少四次能够等待轮流达三十秒。
Mùbiāo: Zài sì zhōu nèi, értóng jiāng zài xūyào shí shǐyòng shìjué tíshì, bìng zài wǔ cì guānchá jīhuì zhōng zhìshǎo sì cì nénggòu děngdài lúnliú dá sānshí miǎo.
The teacher records progress during games and gradually reduces reminders.
教师在游戏中记录进展，并逐渐减少提醒。
Jiàoshī zài yóuxì zhōng jìlù jìnzhǎn, bìng zhújiàn jiǎnshǎo tíxǐng.
This is an illustrative teaching goal, not a universal developmental standard.
这是一个教学目标示例，并不是适用于所有儿童的发展标准。
Zhè shì yí gè jiàoxué mùbiāo shìlì, bìng bú shì shìyòng yú suǒyǒu értóng de fāzhǎn biāozhǔn.
