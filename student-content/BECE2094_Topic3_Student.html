3.1 Detection and Early Intervention and Support (0–3 years old)
3.1 早期发现、早期干预与支持（0–3岁）
3.1 Zǎoqī fāxiàn, zǎoqī gānyù yǔ zhīchí (líng zhì sān suì)
A. What is early detection?
A. 什么是早期发现？
A. Shénme shì zǎoqī fāxiàn?
Early detection means noticing possible developmental difficulties as early as possible.
早期发现是指尽早注意到儿童可能出现的发展困难。
Zǎoqī fāxiàn shì zhǐ jǐnzǎo zhùyì dào értóng kěnéng chūxiàn de fāzhǎn kùnnan.
Young children develop at different speeds.
幼儿的发展速度各不相同。
Yòu'ér de fāzhǎn sùdù gè bù xiāngtóng.
Teachers and caregivers should observe children's development carefully.
教师和照顾者应该仔细观察儿童的发展。
Jiàoshī hé zhàogùzhě yīnggāi zǐxì guānchá értóng de fāzhǎn.
Observation does not mean diagnosing a disability.
观察并不等于诊断残障。
Guānchá bìng bù děngyú zhěnduàn cánzhàng.
B. Five areas of development
B. 五个发展领域
B. Wǔ gè fāzhǎn lǐngyù
1. Physical development
1. 身体发展
1. Shēntǐ fāzhǎn
This includes movement, balance, crawling and walking.
这包括动作、平衡、爬行和走路。
Zhè bāokuò dòngzuò, pínghéng, páxíng hé zǒulù.
2. Language and communication
2. 语言与沟通
2. Yǔyán yǔ gōutōng
This includes understanding words, gestures and expressing needs.
这包括理解词语、手势和表达需要。
Zhè bāokuò lǐjiě cíyǔ, shǒushì hé biǎodá xūyào.
3. Cognitive development
3. 认知发展
3. Rènzhī fāzhǎn
This includes attention, memory, exploration and problem-solving.
这包括注意力、记忆、探索和解决问题。
Zhè bāokuò zhùyìlì, jìyì, tànsuǒ hé jiějué wèntí.
4. Social and emotional development
4. 社交与情绪发展
4. Shèjiāo yǔ qíngxù fāzhǎn
This includes interacting with others and expressing emotions.
这包括与他人互动和表达情绪。
Zhè bāokuò yǔ tārén hùdòng hé biǎodá qíngxù.
5. Self-help development
5. 自理能力发展
5. Zìlǐ nénglì fāzhǎn
This includes feeding, dressing and participating in daily routines.
这包括进食、穿衣和参与日常活动。
Zhè bāokuò jìnshí, chuānyī hé cānyù rìcháng huódòng.
C. Developmental observations
The following are examples of situations that may deserve further observation, not diagnostic criteria.
以下是可能需要进一步观察的情况，而不是诊断标准。
Yǐxià shì kěnéng xūyào jìnyíbù guānchá de qíngkuàng, ér bú shì zhěnduàn biāozhǔn.
Approximate agePossible observation
0–12 monthsLimited response to sounds or difficulty engaging with caregivers
12–24 monthsLimited use of gestures or difficulty understanding familiar instructions
24–36 monthsPersistent difficulty communicating needs or participating in everyday play
A single observation does not confirm a developmental disorder.
一次观察不能确定儿童有发展障碍。
Yí cì guānchá bù néng quèdìng értóng yǒu fāzhǎn zhàng'ài.
Teachers should consider the child's age, language background, opportunities and overall development.
教师应该考虑儿童的年龄、语言背景、成长机会和整体发展。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi kǎolǜ értóng de niánlíng, yǔyán bèijǐng, chéngzhǎng jīhuì hé zhěngtǐ fāzhǎn.
D. What is early intervention?
D. 什么是早期干预？
D. Shénme shì zǎoqī gānyù?
Early intervention means providing suitable support when developmental needs are identified.
早期干预是指在发现发展需要时提供适当的支持。
Zǎoqī gānyù shì zhǐ zài fāxiàn fāzhǎn xūyào shí tígōng shìdàng de zhīchí.
Support may include communication activities, movement activities, family guidance and professional services.
支持可能包括沟通活动、动作活动、家庭指导和专业服务。
Zhīchí kěnéng bāokuò gōutōng huódòng, dòngzuò huódòng, jiātíng zhǐdǎo hé zhuānyè fúwù.
Early support can help children develop skills and participate more confidently.
早期支持可以帮助儿童发展技能，并更有信心地参与活动。
Zǎoqī zhīchí kěyǐ bāngzhù értóng fāzhǎn jìnéng, bìng gèng yǒu xìnxīn de cānyù huódòng.
E. Steps teachers can take
E. 教师可以采取的步骤
E. Jiàoshī kěyǐ cǎiqǔ de bùzhòu
1. Observe the child during everyday activities.
在日常活动中观察儿童。
Zài rìcháng huódòng zhōng guānchá értóng.
2. Record what the child does using factual descriptions.
使用客观描述记录儿童的表现。
Shǐyòng kèguān miáoshù jìlù értóng de biǎoxiàn.
3. Discuss observations respectfully with the family.
以尊重的态度与家庭讨论观察结果。
Yǐ zūnzhòng de tàidù yǔ jiātíng tǎolùn guānchá jiéguǒ.
4. Suggest appropriate professional assessment or referral when needed.
在需要时建议适当的专业评估或转介。
Zài xūyào shí jiànyì shìdàng de zhuānyè pínggū huò zhuǎnjiè.
5. Continue providing suitable classroom support.
继续提供适当的课堂支持。
Jìxù tígōng shìdàng de kètáng zhīchí.

3.2 Strategies for Inclusive Education in Nursery and Kindergarten
3.2 托儿所与幼儿园的融合教育策略
3.2 Tuō'érsuǒ yǔ yòu'éryuán de rónghé jiàoyù cèlüè
A. What is inclusive education?
A. 什么是融合教育？
A. Shénme shì rónghé jiàoyù?
Inclusive education means helping children with different abilities learn and play together.
融合教育是帮助不同能力的儿童一起学习和游戏。
Rónghé jiàoyù shì bāngzhù bùtóng nénglì de értóng yìqǐ xuéxí hé yóuxì.
Children may need different kinds of support to participate.
儿童可能需要不同类型的支持才能参与活动。
Értóng kěnéng xūyào bùtóng lèixíng de zhīchí cái néng cānyù huódòng.
B. Six inclusive teaching strategies
B. 六种融合教学策略
B. Liù zhǒng rónghé jiàoxué cèlüè
1. Use visual supports
1. 使用视觉辅助工具
1. Shǐyòng shìjué fǔzhù gōngjù
Pictures, symbols and schedules can help children understand instructions.
图片、符号和时间表可以帮助儿童理解指示。
Túpiàn, fúhào hé shíjiānbiǎo kěyǐ bāngzhù értóng lǐjiě zhǐshì.
Example: Show pictures for washing hands, eating and resting.
例子： 展示洗手、进食和休息的图片。
Lìzi: Zhǎnshì xǐshǒu, jìnshí hé xiūxi de túpiàn.
2. Give simple instructions
2. 提供简单指示
2. Tígōng jiǎndān zhǐshì
Give one instruction at a time when needed.
在需要时，一次只给予一项指示。
Zài xūyào shí, yí cì zhǐ jǐyǔ yí xiàng zhǐshì.
Example: Say “Put the block in the box” and demonstrate the action.
例子： 说“把积木放进盒子里”，并示范动作。
Lìzi: Shuō “Bǎ jīmù fàng jìn hézi lǐ”, bìng shìfàn dòngzuò.
3. Use peer support
3. 使用同伴支持
3. Shǐyòng tóngbàn zhīchí
Children can learn through positive interactions with classmates.
儿童可以通过与同伴的积极互动来学习。
Értóng kěyǐ tōngguò yǔ tóngbàn de jījí hùdòng lái xuéxí.
Example: Pair children to build a tower together.
例子： 安排两名儿童一起搭建积木塔。
Lìzi: Ānpái liǎng míng értóng yìqǐ dājiàn jīmù tǎ.
4. Offer different ways to participate
4. 提供不同的参与方式
4. Tígōng bùtóng de cānyù fāngshì
A child may point, speak, move or use a communication aid.
儿童可以通过指认、说话、动作或沟通辅助工具参与。
Értóng kěyǐ tōngguò zhǐrèn, shuōhuà, dòngzuò huò gōutōng fǔzhù gōngjù cānyù.
Example: During storytelling, a child can point to the correct picture instead of speaking.
例子： 在讲故事时，儿童可以指向正确的图片，而不一定要说话。
Lìzi: Zài jiǎng gùshi shí, értóng kěyǐ zhǐ xiàng zhèngquè de túpiàn, ér bù yídìng yào shuōhuà.
5. Create accessible environments
5. 创造无障碍环境
5. Chuàngzào wú zhàng'ài huánjìng
Arrange furniture and materials so children can move and participate safely.
安排家具和材料，让儿童能够安全地移动和参与。
Ānpái jiājù hé cáiliào, ràng értóng nénggòu ānquán de yídòng hé cānyù.
6. Work with families
6. 与家庭合作
6. Yǔ jiātíng hézuò
Families can share information about children's interests, communication and support needs.
家庭可以分享儿童的兴趣、沟通方式和支持需要。
Jiātíng kěyǐ fēnxiǎng értóng de xìngqù, gōutōng fāngshì hé zhīchí xūyào.
C. Malaysia and China examples
C. 马来西亚与中国的例子
C. Mǎláixīyà yǔ Zhōngguó de lìzi
Malaysia: A kindergarten uses picture cards in Malay, English or Mandarin, according to children's language needs.
马来西亚： 一所幼儿园根据儿童的语言需要，使用马来语、英语或华语图片卡。
Mǎláixīyà: Yì suǒ yòu'éryuán gēnjù értóng de yǔyán xūyào, shǐyòng Mǎláiyǔ, Yīngyǔ huò Huáyǔ túpiàn kǎ.
China: A kindergarten uses visual schedules and small-group activities to support children who need additional guidance.
中国： 一所幼儿园使用视觉时间表和小组活动，支持需要额外指导的儿童。
Zhōngguó: Yì suǒ yòu'éryuán shǐyòng shìjué shíjiānbiǎo hé xiǎozǔ huódòng, zhīchí xūyào éwài zhǐdǎo de értóng.

3.3 Basic Principles for Modifying Activities for Special Needs
3.3 为特殊需要调整活动的基本原则
3.3 Wèi tèshū xūyào tiáozhěng huódòng de jīběn yuánzé
A. What is activity modification?
A. 什么是活动调整？
A. Shénme shì huódòng tiáozhěng?
Activity modification means changing materials, instructions, time or participation methods to meet children's needs.
活动调整是指改变材料、指示、时间或参与方式，以满足儿童的需要。
Huódòng tiáozhěng shì zhǐ gǎibiàn cáiliào, zhǐshì, shíjiān huò cānyù fāngshì, yǐ mǎnzú értóng de xūyào.
The purpose is to remove unnecessary barriers to learning.
目的是消除学习中不必要的障碍。
Mùdì shì xiāochú xuéxí zhōng bù bìyào de zhàng'ài.
B. Five basic principles
B. 五项基本原则
B. Wǔ xiàng jīběn yuánzé
1. Start with the child's strengths
1. 从儿童的优势开始
1. Cóng értóng de yōushì kāishǐ
Use activities that connect with what the child can already do.
使用与儿童现有能力相关的活动。
Shǐyòng yǔ értóng xiànyǒu nénglì xiāngguān de huódòng.
2. Keep learning goals meaningful
2. 保持学习目标有意义
2. Bǎochí xuéxí mùbiāo yǒu yìyì
Adjust the task while keeping an appropriate learning purpose.
调整任务，同时保留适当的学习目的。
Tiáozhěng rènwu, tóngshí bǎoliú shìdàng de xuéxí mùdì.
3. Change materials when needed
3. 按需要调整材料
3. Àn xūyào tiáozhěng cáiliào
Use larger objects, thicker crayons or textured materials.
使用较大的物品、较粗的蜡笔或有触感的材料。
Shǐyòng jiào dà de wùpǐn, jiào cū de làbǐ huò yǒu chùgǎn de cáiliào.
4. Allow flexible participation
4. 允许灵活参与
4. Yǔnxǔ línghuó cānyù
Children may demonstrate learning through different actions.
儿童可以通过不同的动作展示学习成果。
Értóng kěyǐ tōngguò bùtóng de dòngzuò zhǎnshì xuéxí chéngguǒ.
5. Observe and review
5. 观察与检讨
5. Guānchá yǔ jiǎntǎo
Check whether the modification helps the child participate and learn.
检查活动调整是否帮助儿童参与和学习。
Jiǎnchá huódòng tiáozhěng shìfǒu bāngzhù értóng cānyù hé xuéxí.
C. Example: Modifying one activity
C. 例子：调整同一项活动
C. Lìzi: Tiáozhěng tóng yí xiàng huódòng
Original activity: Sorting coloured blocks
原本活动：分类彩色积木
Yuánběn huódòng: Fēnlèi cǎisè jīmù
Learning goal: Sort blocks by colour.
学习目标：根据颜色分类积木。
Xuéxí mùbiāo: Gēnjù yánsè fēnlèi jīmù.
Child's support needPossible modification
Difficulty grasping small objectsUse larger blocks
Difficulty understanding spoken instructionsDemonstrate sorting and use picture cues
Difficulty distinguishing certain coloursUse high-contrast choices and check that colours are distinguishable
Limited mobilityPosition materials within comfortable reach
Needs additional processing timeReduce distractions and allow more time
All children can work towards a suitable colour-sorting goal.
所有儿童都可以朝着适当的颜色分类目标学习。
Suǒyǒu értóng dōu kěyǐ cháozhe shìdàng de yánsè fēnlèi mùbiāo xuéxí.
However, if a child cannot reliably distinguish the chosen colours, the teacher should reconsider the goal or assessment rather than treating the difficulty as failure.
但是，如果儿童无法可靠地区分所选颜色，教师应该重新考虑目标或评估方式，而不是把困难视为失败。
Dànshì, rúguǒ értóng wúfǎ kěkào de qūfēn suǒ xuǎn yánsè, jiàoshī yīnggāi chóngxīn kǎolǜ mùbiāo huò pínggū fāngshì, ér bú shì bǎ kùnnan shìwéi shībài.
Hands-on Activity 3.3 — Modify the Activity
实践活动3.3——调整活动
Shíjiàn huódòng sān diǎn sān — Tiáozhěng huódòng
1. Choose a preschool activity such as drawing, singing or block play.
选择一项幼儿园活动，例如画画、唱歌或积木游戏。
Xuǎnzé yí xiàng yòu'éryuán huódòng, lìrú huàhuà, chànggē huò jīmù yóuxì.
2. Identify two possible barriers to participation.
找出两个可能影响参与的障碍。
Zhǎo chū liǎng gè kěnéng yǐngxiǎng cānyù de zhàng'ài.

3.3 Individualised Educational Plan (IEP)
3.3 个别化教育计划（IEP）
3.3 Gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà (IEP)
A. What is an IEP?
A. 什么是个别化教育计划？
A. Shénme shì gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà?
An Individualised Educational Plan is a written plan describing a child's learning needs, goals and support strategies.
个别化教育计划是一份书面计划，说明儿童的学习需要、目标和支持策略。
Gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà shì yí fèn shūmiàn jìhuà, shuōmíng értóng de xuéxí xūyào, mùbiāo hé zhīchí cèlüè.
An IEP helps teachers plan, monitor and review individual learning progress.
个别化教育计划帮助教师规划、观察和检讨个人学习进展。
Gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà bāngzhù jiàoshī guīhuà, guānchá hé jiǎntǎo gèrén xuéxí jìnzhǎn.
The exact requirements and procedures for an IEP depend on the education system and setting.
个别化教育计划的具体要求和程序取决于教育制度及教育环境。
Gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà de jùtǐ yāoqiú hé chéngxù qǔjué yú jiàoyù zhìdù jí jiàoyù huánjìng.
B. Important components of an IEP
B. 个别化教育计划的重要组成部分
B. Gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà de zhòngyào zǔchéng bùfen
1. Present level of development
1. 目前的发展水平
1. Mùqián de fāzhǎn shuǐpíng
Describe what the child can currently do.
描述儿童目前能够做到的事情。
Miáoshù értóng mùqián nénggòu zuò dào de shìqing.
2. Learning goal
2. 学习目标
2. Xuéxí mùbiāo
State a clear, measurable and realistic goal.
设定一个清楚、可衡量且实际的目标。
Shèdìng yí gè qīngchǔ, kě héngliáng qiě shíjì de mùbiāo.
3. Teaching strategies
3. 教学策略
3. Jiàoxué cèlüè
Explain how teachers will support the child.
解释教师将如何支持儿童。
Jiěshì jiàoshī jiāng rúhé zhīchí értóng.
4. Resources and support
4. 资源与支持
4. Zīyuán yǔ zhīchí
List the materials, people and services needed.
列出所需的材料、人员和服务。
Lièchū suǒ xū de cáiliào, rényuán hé fúwù.
5. Progress monitoring
5. 进展观察
5. Jìnzhǎn guānchá
Record progress using observations and simple assessments.
使用观察和简单评估记录进展。
Shǐyòng guānchá hé jiǎndān pínggū jìlù jìnzhǎn.
6. Review
6. 检讨
6. Jiǎntǎo
Review the plan and adjust support when necessary.
检讨计划，并在需要时调整支持。
Jiǎntǎo jìhuà, bìng zài xūyào shí tiáozhěng zhīchí.
C. Example of a simple IEP
C. 简单的个别化教育计划例子
C. Jiǎndān de gèbiéhuà jiàoyù jìhuà lìzi

虚构个案：五岁的美美
Xūgòu gè'àn: Wǔ suì de Měiměi
Mei enjoys picture books but finds it difficult to communicate her choices verbally.
美美喜欢图画书，但难以用口语表达自己的选择。

StrengthEnjoys pictures and responds to visual choices
Current performanceIndependently selects a preferred picture in 1 of 5 observed opportunities
GoalWithin six weeks, Mei will independently choose between two picture options in at least 4 of 5 opportunities across three consecutive sessions
StrategiesPicture-choice board, modelling and sufficient response time
ResourcesLaminated picture cards and choice board
MonitoringRecord independent choices during each session
ReviewReview progress after six weeks
Learning goal
学习目标
Xuéxí mùbiāo
Within six weeks, Mei will independently choose between two pictures in at least four out of five opportunities across three consecutive sessions.
在六周内，美美将在连续三次活动中，每次至少五次机会里有四次能够独立从两张图片中作出选择。
Zài liù zhōu nèi, Měiměi jiāng zài liánxù sān cì huódòng zhōng, měi cì zhìshǎo wǔ cì jīhuì lǐ yǒu sì cì nénggòu dúlì cóng liǎng zhāng túpiàn zhōng zuòchū xuǎnzé.

这个目标是可衡量的，因为教师可以计算儿童成功独立选择的次数。
Zhège mùbiāo shì kě héngliáng de, yīnwèi jiàoshī kěyǐ jìsuàn értóng chénggōng dúlì xuǎnzé de cìshù.

重要提醒
Zhòngyào tíxǐng

个别化教育计划应该根据当地要求，与家庭和相关专业人员合作制定。

3.4 Planning and Instructional Strategies in Special Education
3.4 特殊教育的教学规划与教学策略

A. Why is planning important?
A. 为什么教学规划很重要？
A. Wèishénme jiàoxué guīhuà hěn zhòngyào?
Planning helps teachers prepare suitable activities for children with different abilities.
教学规划帮助教师为不同能力的儿童准备适当的活动。
Jiàoxué guīhuà bāngzhù jiàoshī wèi bùtóng nénglì de értóng zhǔnbèi shìdàng de huódòng.
Good planning includes clear goals, suitable materials, flexible teaching and progress monitoring.
良好的教学规划包括清楚的目标、适当的材料、灵活的教学和进展观察。
Liánghǎo de jiàoxué guīhuà bāokuò qīngchǔ de mùbiāo, shìdàng de cáiliào, línghuó de jiàoxué hé jìnzhǎn guānchá.
B. Five useful instructional strategies
B. 五种实用的教学策略
B. Wǔ zhǒng shíyòng de jiàoxué cèlüè
1. Modelling
1. 示范教学
1. Shìfàn jiàoxué
Show the child how to complete a task before asking them to try.
先向儿童示范如何完成任务，再请他们尝试。
Xiān xiàng értóng shìfàn rúhé wánchéng rènwu, zài qǐng tāmen chángshì.
2. Task analysis
2. 任务分析
2. Rènwu fēnxī
Break a difficult task into smaller steps.
把困难的任务分成较小的步骤。
Bǎ kùnnan de rènwu fēn chéng jiào xiǎo de bùzhòu.
Example: Washing hands can be taught through turning on the tap, wetting hands, using soap, rinsing and drying.
例子： 可以通过开水龙头、弄湿双手、使用肥皂、冲洗和擦干来教儿童洗手。
Lìzi: Kěyǐ tōngguò kāi shuǐlóngtóu, nòng shī shuāngshǒu, shǐyòng féizào, chōngxǐ hé cāgān lái jiāo értóng xǐshǒu.
3. Prompting
3. 提示
3. Tíshì
Use appropriate verbal, visual or gestural prompts when needed.
在需要时使用适当的口头、视觉或手势提示。
Zài xūyào shí shǐyòng shìdàng de kǒutóu, shìjué huò shǒushì tíshì.
Gradually reduce prompts as the child becomes more independent.
随着儿童越来越独立，逐渐减少提示。
Suízhe értóng yuè lái yuè dúlì, zhújiàn jiǎnshǎo tíshì.
4. Positive reinforcement
4. 正向强化
4. Zhèngxiàng qiánghuà
Recognise and encourage useful efforts and skills.
认可并鼓励有帮助的努力和技能。
Rènkě bìng gǔlì yǒu bāngzhù de nǔlì hé jìnéng.
Example: Say, “You put the toys away by yourself. Well done!”
例子： 说：“你自己把玩具收好了，做得好！”
Lìzi: Shuō: “Nǐ zìjǐ bǎ wánjù shōu hǎo le, zuò de hǎo!”
5. Play-based learning
5. 游戏式学习
5. Yóuxì shì xuéxí
Use meaningful play to develop communication, movement and thinking skills.
通过有意义的游戏发展沟通、动作和思考能力。
Tōngguò yǒu yìyì de yóuxì fāzhǎn gōutōng, dòngzuò hé sīkǎo nénglì.

C. 例子：规划融合教学活动

Teaching steps

说出水果名称，并让儿童安全地探索水果。
Shuō chū shuǐguǒ míngchēng, bìng ràng értóng ānquán de tànsuǒ shuǐguǒ.

请儿童通过说话、指认或选择图片来识别水果。

在需要时提供额外的视觉或身体动作支持。
