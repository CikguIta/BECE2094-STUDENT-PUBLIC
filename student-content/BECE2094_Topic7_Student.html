A. What is Autism Spectrum Disorder?
A. 什么是孤独症谱系障碍？
A. Shénme shì gūdúzhèng pǔxì zhàng'ài?
Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental condition involving differences in social communication and interaction, together with restricted or repetitive patterns of behaviour, interests or activities.
孤独症谱系障碍（ASD）是一种神经发育状况，涉及社交沟通和互动方面的差异，以及局限或重复的行为、兴趣或活动模式。
Gūdúzhèng pǔxì zhàng'ài (ASD) shì yì zhǒng shénjīng fāyù zhuàngkuàng, shèjí shèjiāo gōutōng hé hùdòng fāngmiàn de chāyì, yǐjí júxiàn huò chóngfù de xíngwéi, xìngqù huò huódòng móshì.
Autistic children may also experience sensory differences involving sound, light, touch, movement or other sensations.
孤独症儿童也可能在声音、光线、触觉、动作或其他感觉方面有不同的感官体验。
Gūdúzhèng értóng yě kěnéng zài shēngyīn, guāngxiàn, chùjué, dòngzuò huò qítā gǎnjué fāngmiàn yǒu bùtóng de gǎnguān tǐyàn.
Autism is a spectrum, meaning that each autistic child has a different combination of strengths, characteristics and support needs.
孤独症是一个谱系，意味着每名孤独症儿童都有不同的优势、特征和支持需要。
Gūdúzhèng shì yí gè pǔxì, yìwèizhe měi míng gūdúzhèng értóng dōu yǒu bùtóng de yōushì, tèzhēng hé zhīchí xūyào.
Autism is not caused by poor parenting, and it should not be understood as a lack of intelligence or affection.
孤独症不是由不良教养造成的，也不应该被理解为缺乏智力或感情。
Gūdúzhèng bú shì yóu bùliáng jiàoyǎng zàochéng de, yě bù yīnggāi bèi lǐjiě wéi quēfá zhìlì huò gǎnqíng.
B. Common characteristics
B. 常见特征
B. Chángjiàn tèzhēng
1. Differences in social communication
1. 社交沟通方面的差异
Yī. Shèjiāo gōutōng fāngmiàn de chāyì
Some children may use fewer spoken words, interpret language literally or communicate through gestures, pictures or communication devices.
一些儿童可能使用较少的口语、按字面意思理解语言，或通过手势、图片或沟通设备交流。
Yìxiē értóng kěnéng shǐyòng jiào shǎo de kǒuyǔ, àn zìmiàn yìsi lǐjiě yǔyán, huò tōngguò shǒushì, túpiàn huò gōutōng shèbèi jiāoliú.
2. Differences in social interaction
2. 社交互动方面的差异
Èr. Shèjiāo hùdòng fāngmiàn de chāyì
Some children may prefer independent play, need support to join group activities or interact with peers in different ways.
一些儿童可能喜欢独自游戏、需要支持才能参与小组活动，或以不同方式与同伴互动。
Yìxiē értóng kěnéng xǐhuān dúzì yóuxì, xūyào zhīchí cái néng cānyù xiǎozǔ huódòng, huò yǐ bùtóng fāngshì yǔ tóngbàn hùdòng.
3. Repetitive behaviours and focused interests
3. 重复行为和专注的兴趣
Sān. Chóngfù xíngwéi hé zhuānzhù de xìngqù
A child may repeat movements, enjoy familiar routines or show strong interest in particular objects or topics.
儿童可能重复某些动作、喜欢熟悉的日常程序，或对特定物品或主题表现出强烈兴趣。
Értóng kěnéng chóngfù mǒuxiē dòngzuò, xǐhuān shúxī de rìcháng chéngxù, huò duì tèdìng wùpǐn huò zhǔtí biǎoxiàn chū qiángliè xìngqù.
4. Sensory differences
4. 感官体验差异
Sì. Gǎnguān tǐyàn chāyì
A child may be very sensitive to loud sounds or certain textures, or may seek particular sensory experiences.
儿童可能对大声响或某些材质非常敏感，也可能主动寻求特定的感官体验。
Értóng kěnéng duì dà shēngxiǎng huò mǒuxiē cáizhì fēicháng mǐngǎn, yě kěnéng zhǔdòng xúnqiú tèdìng de gǎnguān tǐyàn.
5. Differences in flexibility and transitions
5. 适应变化和活动转换方面的差异
Wǔ. Shìyìng biànhuà hé huódòng zhuǎnhuàn fāngmiàn de chāyì
Some children may feel distressed when routines change unexpectedly or when moving between activities.
一些儿童在日常程序突然改变或转换活动时可能感到不安。
Yìxiē értóng zài rìcháng chéngxù tūrán gǎibiàn huò zhuǎnhuàn huódòng shí kěnéng gǎndào bù'ān.
These characteristics vary between children and may change across situations and stages of development.
这些特征因儿童而异，也可能随着情境和发展阶段而改变。
Zhèxiē tèzhēng yīn értóng ér yì, yě kěnéng suízhe qíngjìng hé fāzhǎn jiēduàn ér gǎibiàn.
C. Educational considerations
C. 教育方面的考量
C. Jiàoyù fāngmiàn de kǎoliáng
1. Recognise individual strengths
1. 认识个人优势
Yī. Rènshi gèrén yōushì
Identify the child's interests, preferred ways of learning and existing abilities.
识别儿童的兴趣、偏好的学习方式和已有能力。
Shíbié értóng de xìngqù, piānhào de xuéxí fāngshì hé yǐyǒu nénglì.
2. Support different communication methods
2. 支持不同的沟通方式
Èr. Zhīchí bùtóng de gōutōng fāngshì
Accept spoken language, gestures, pictures and augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) as meaningful communication.
接受口语、手势、图片以及辅助与替代沟通（AAC）作为有意义的沟通方式。
Jiēshòu kǒuyǔ, shǒushì, túpiàn yǐjí fǔzhù yǔ tìdài gōutōng (AAC) zuòwéi yǒu yìyì de gōutōng fāngshì.
3. Consider sensory comfort
3. 考虑感官舒适度
Sān. Kǎolǜ gǎnguān shūshìdù
Reduce unnecessary sensory stress and offer suitable choices when possible.
减少不必要的感官压力，并在可能时提供适当选择。
Jiǎnshǎo bù bìyào de gǎnguān yālì, bìng zài kěnéng shí tígōng shìdàng xuǎnzé.
4. Provide predictability
4. 提供可预测性
Sì. Tígōng kě yùcè xìng
Use visual schedules, consistent routines and advance warnings of changes.
使用视觉时间表、一致的日常程序，以及提前通知变化。
Shǐyòng shìjué shíjiānbiǎo, yízhì de rìcháng chéngxù, yǐjí tíqián tōngzhī biànhuà.
5. Respect autonomy and dignity
5. 尊重自主权和尊严
Wǔ. Zūnzhòng zìzhǔquán hé zūnyán
Do not force eye contact, physical touch or unnecessary suppression of harmless self-regulating movements.
不要强迫眼神接触、身体接触，或不必要地制止无害的自我调节动作。
Bú yào qiǎngpò yǎnshén jiēchù, shēntǐ jiēchù, huò bù bìyào de zhìzhǐ wúhài de zìwǒ tiáojié dòngzuò.
6. Observe before making conclusions
6. 先观察，再作出结论
Liù. Xiān guānchá, zài zuòchū jiélùn
Teachers should document learning and behaviour patterns, discuss concerns respectfully with families and seek appropriate professional support when needed.
教师应该记录学习和行为模式、尊重地与家庭讨论关注事项，并在需要时寻求适当的专业支持。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi jìlù xuéxí hé xíngwéi móshì, zūnzhòng de yǔ jiātíng tǎolùn guānzhù shìxiàng, bìng zài xūyào shí xúnqiú shìdàng de zhuānyè zhīchí.
Teachers do not diagnose autism. Diagnosis requires assessment by appropriately qualified professionals.
教师不负责诊断孤独症。诊断需要由具备适当资格的专业人员进行评估。
Jiàoshī bù fùzé zhěnduàn gūdúzhèng. Zhěnduàn xūyào yóu jùbèi shìdàng zīgé de zhuānyè rényuán jìnxíng pínggū.

7.2 Early Intervention
7.2 早期干预
7.2 Zǎoqī gānyù
A. What is early intervention?
A. 什么是早期干预？
A. Shénme shì zǎoqī gānyù?
Early intervention provides individualised support to help young autistic children develop communication, play, everyday living and participation skills.
早期干预提供个别化支持，帮助孤独症幼儿发展沟通、游戏、日常生活和参与活动的技能。
Zǎoqī gānyù tígōng gèbiéhuà zhīchí, bāngzhù gūdúzhèng yòu'ér fāzhǎn gōutōng, yóuxì, rìcháng shēnghuó hé cānyù huódòng de jìnéng.
Early intervention should be based on the child's needs, strengths, family priorities and developmental level.
早期干预应该根据儿童的需要、优势、家庭的优先事项和发展水平来规划。
Zǎoqī gānyù yīnggāi gēnjù értóng de xūyào, yōushì, jiātíng de yōuxiān shìxiàng hé fāzhǎn shuǐpíng lái guīhuà.
Support for communication and participation can begin before a formal diagnosis.
沟通和参与方面的支持可以在正式诊断之前开始。
Gōutōng hé cānyù fāngmiàn de zhīchí kěyǐ zài zhèngshì zhěnduàn zhīqián kāishǐ.
B. Main areas of early intervention
B. 早期干预的主要领域
B. Zǎoqī gānyù de zhǔyào lǐngyù
1. Functional communication
1. 功能性沟通
Yī. Gōngnéngxìng gōutōng
Help children express needs, make choices, request assistance and share interests using suitable communication methods.
帮助儿童使用适当的沟通方式表达需要、作出选择、寻求帮助和分享兴趣。
Bāngzhù értóng shǐyòng shìdàng de gōutōng fāngshì biǎodá xūyào, zuòchū xuǎnzé, xúnqiú bāngzhù hé fēnxiǎng xìngqù.
For example, a child may point to a picture of water to request a drink.
例如，儿童可以指向水的图片来表示想喝水。
Lìrú, értóng kěyǐ zhǐ xiàng shuǐ de túpiàn lái biǎoshì xiǎng hē shuǐ.
2. Play and interaction
2. 游戏与互动
Èr. Yóuxì yǔ hùdòng
Use enjoyable, child-led play to support shared activities, communication and learning.
使用愉快、由儿童主导的游戏来支持共同活动、沟通和学习。
Shǐyòng yúkuài, yóu értóng zhǔdǎo de yóuxì lái zhīchí gòngtóng huódòng, gōutōng hé xuéxí.
3. Everyday living skills
3. 日常生活技能
Sān. Rìcháng shēnghuó jìnéng
Teach useful skills such as washing hands, organising belongings and asking for help.
教授洗手、整理个人物品和寻求帮助等实用技能。
Jiàoshòu xǐshǒu, zhěnglǐ gèrén wùpǐn hé xúnqiú bāngzhù děng shíyòng jìnéng.
4. Emotional and sensory regulation
4. 情绪与感官调节
Sì. Qíngxù yǔ gǎnguān tiáojié
Help children recognise discomfort and access suitable ways to feel safe and calm.
帮助儿童识别不适，并使用适当的方法获得安全感和平静。
Bāngzhù értóng shíbié bùshì, bìng shǐyòng shìdàng de fāngfǎ huòdé ānquángǎn hé píngjìng.
5. Family involvement
5. 家庭参与
Wǔ. Jiātíng cānyù
Work with families to select meaningful goals that can be practised during familiar routines.
与家庭合作选择有意义的目标，并在熟悉的日常活动中练习。
Yǔ jiātíng hézuò xuǎnzé yǒu yìyì de mùbiāo, bìng zài shúxī de rìcháng huódòng zhōng liànxí.
