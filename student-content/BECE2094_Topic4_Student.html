A. Understanding intellectual and developmental disabilities
What is an intellectual disability?
什么是智力障碍？
Shénme shì zhìlì zhàng'ài?
An intellectual disability involves significant limitations in intellectual functioning and adaptive behaviour that begin during the developmental period.
智力障碍是指在发展时期出现的智力功能和适应性行为方面的明显限制。
Zhìlì zhàng'ài shì zhǐ zài fāzhǎn shíqī chūxiàn de zhìlì gōngnéng hé shìyìngxìng xíngwéi fāngmiàn de míngxiǎn xiànzhì.
Intellectual functioning includes learning, reasoning and problem-solving.
智力功能包括学习、推理和解决问题。
Zhìlì gōngnéng bāokuò xuéxí, tuīlǐ hé jiějué wèntí.
Adaptive behaviour includes everyday skills such as communication, self-care and social participation.
适应性行为包括沟通、自我照顾和社交参与等日常技能。
Shìyìngxìng xíngwéi bāokuò gōutōng, zìwǒ zhàogù hé shèjiāo cānyù děng rìcháng jìnéng.
What is a developmental disability?
什么是发展障碍？
Shénme shì fāzhǎn zhàng'ài?
Developmental disabilities are a broader group of conditions that begin during development and may affect physical, learning, language or behavioural functioning.
发展障碍是一组范围较广的状况，始于发展时期，并可能影响身体、学习、语言或行为功能。
Fāzhǎn zhàng'ài shì yì zǔ fànwéi jiào guǎng de zhuàngkuàng, shǐ yú fāzhǎn shíqī, bìng kěnéng yǐngxiǎng shēntǐ, xuéxí, yǔyán huò xíngwéi gōngnéng.
Some children have both an intellectual disability and another developmental disability.
有些儿童同时有智力障碍和其他发展障碍。
Yǒuxiē értóng tóngshí yǒu zhìlì zhàng'ài hé qítā fāzhǎn zhàng'ài.
However, a developmental disability does not always involve an intellectual disability.
但是，发展障碍并不一定涉及智力障碍。
Dànshì, fāzhǎn zhàng'ài bìng bù yídìng shèjí zhìlì zhàng'ài.
B. Examples of developmental disabilities
1. Intellectual disability
1. 智力障碍
Yī. Zhìlì zhàng'ài
A child may need additional time, repeated practice and support to learn everyday skills.
儿童可能需要额外时间、重复练习和支持来学习日常技能。
Értóng kěnéng xūyào éwài shíjiān, chóngfù liànxí hé zhīchí lái xuéxí rìcháng jìnéng.
2. Autism spectrum disorder (ASD)
2. 孤独症谱系障碍（ASD）
Èr. Gūdúzhèng pǔxì zhàng'ài
An autistic child may have different communication, social interaction or sensory needs.
孤独症儿童可能有不同的沟通、社交互动或感官需要。
Gūdúzhèng értóng kěnéng yǒu bùtóng de gōutōng, shèjiāo hùdòng huò gǎnguān xūyào.
Autism does not automatically mean that a child has an intellectual disability.
孤独症并不自动意味着儿童有智力障碍。
Gūdúzhèng bìng bù zìdòng yìwèizhe értóng yǒu zhìlì zhàng'ài.
3. Down syndrome
3. 唐氏综合征
Sān. Tángshì zōnghézhēng
Some children with Down syndrome may need additional support for language, learning, movement and self-care.
一些唐氏综合征儿童可能需要额外的语言、学习、动作和自理能力支持。
Yìxiē Tángshì zōnghézhēng értóng kěnéng xūyào éwài de yǔyán, xuéxí, dòngzuò hé zìlǐ nénglì zhīchí.
Children with Down syndrome have different strengths, personalities and learning needs.
唐氏综合征儿童各有不同的优势、个性和学习需要。
Tángshì zōnghézhēng értóng gè yǒu bùtóng de yōushì, gèxìng hé xuéxí xūyào.
4. Cerebral palsy
4. 脑性瘫痪
Sì. Nǎoxìng tānhuàn
Cerebral palsy mainly affects movement and posture.
脑性瘫痪主要影响动作和姿势。
Nǎoxìng tānhuàn zhǔyào yǐngxiǎng dòngzuò hé zīshì.
A child with cerebral palsy does not necessarily have an intellectual disability.
脑性瘫痪儿童不一定有智力障碍。
Nǎoxìng tānhuàn értóng bù yídìng yǒu zhìlì zhàng'ài.

4.2 Early Intervention
4.2 早期干预
4.2 Zǎoqī gānyù
A. What is early intervention?
Early intervention means providing suitable support to young children who have identified developmental needs or are at risk of developmental difficulties.
早期干预是指为已发现有发展需要或可能出现发展困难的幼儿提供适当支持。
Zǎoqī gānyù shì zhǐ wèi yǐ fāxiàn yǒu fāzhǎn xūyào huò kěnéng chūxiàn fāzhǎn kùnnan de yòu'ér tígōng shìdàng zhīchí.
It may involve families, teachers, healthcare professionals and other specialists.
早期干预可能涉及家庭、教师、医疗专业人员和其他专家。
Zǎoqī gānyù kěnéng shèjí jiātíng, jiàoshī, yīliáo zhuānyè rényuán hé qítā zhuānjiā.
Early intervention does not require teachers to wait for a formal diagnosis before offering ordinary, appropriate classroom support.
早期干预不要求教师等到正式诊断后，才提供一般而适当的课堂支持。
Zǎoqī gānyù bù yāoqiú jiàoshī děng dào zhèngshì zhěnduàn hòu, cái tígōng yìbān ér shìdàng de kètáng zhīchí.
B. Why is early intervention important?
1. Supports communication
1. 支持沟通
Yī. Zhīchí gōutōng
Early communication activities help children express needs and interact with others.
早期沟通活动帮助儿童表达需要，并与他人互动。
Zǎoqī gōutōng huódòng bāngzhù értóng biǎodá xūyào, bìng yǔ tārén hùdòng.
2. Develops daily living skills
2. 发展日常生活技能
Èr. Fāzhǎn rìcháng shēnghuó jìnéng
Children can practise feeding, dressing, handwashing and other everyday activities.
儿童可以练习进食、穿衣、洗手和其他日常活动。
Értóng kěyǐ liànxí jìnshí, chuānyī, xǐshǒu hé qítā rìcháng huódòng.
3. Encourages learning through play
3. 鼓励通过游戏学习
Sān. Gǔlì tōngguò yóuxì xuéxí
Play can support attention, communication, movement and problem-solving.
游戏可以支持注意力、沟通、动作和解决问题的能力。
Yóuxì kěyǐ zhīchí zhùyìlì, gōutōng, dòngzuò hé jiějué wèntí de nénglì.
4. Supports families
4. 支持家庭
Sì. Zhīchí jiātíng
Families can learn practical strategies to support children during daily routines.
家庭可以学习实际策略，在日常生活中支持儿童。
Jiātíng kěyǐ xuéxí shíjì cèlüè, zài rìcháng shēnghuó zhōng zhīchí értóng.
C. Five early intervention strategies
1. Play-based intervention
1. 游戏式干预
Yī. Yóuxì shì gānyù
Use simple games to practise communication, movement and thinking skills.
使用简单游戏来练习沟通、动作和思考技能。
Shǐyòng jiǎndān yóuxì lái liànxí gōutōng, dòngzuò hé sīkǎo jìnéng.

2. Visual support
2. 视觉支持
Èr. Shìjué zhīchí
Use picture schedules, symbols and demonstrations to explain routines.
使用图片时间表、符号和示范来解释日常程序。
Shǐyòng túpiàn shíjiānbiǎo, fúhào hé shìfàn lái jiěshì rìcháng chéngxù.
3. Small-step teaching
3. 小步骤教学
Sān. Xiǎo bùzhòu jiàoxué
Divide a skill into small, manageable steps.
把一项技能分成较小、容易掌握的步骤。
Bǎ yí xiàng jìnéng fēn chéng jiào xiǎo, róngyì zhǎngwò de bùzhòu.
4. Communication support
4. 沟通支持
Sì. Gōutōng zhīchí
Accept appropriate gestures, pictures, signs or communication devices as ways to express needs.
接受适当的手势、图片、手语或沟通设备作为表达需要的方式。
Jiēshòu shìdàng de shǒushì, túpiàn, shǒuyǔ huò gōutōng shèbèi zuòwéi biǎodá xūyào de fāngshì.
5. Family-centred support
5. 以家庭为中心的支持
Wǔ. Yǐ jiātíng wéi zhōngxīn de zhīchí
Work with families to choose useful goals and practise skills in everyday situations.
与家庭合作选择有用的目标，并在日常生活中练习技能。
Yǔ jiātíng hézuò xuǎnzé yǒuyòng de mùbiāo, bìng zài rìcháng shēnghuó zhōng liànxí jìnéng.

4.3 Programme Differentiation
4.3 课程差异化
4.3 Kèchéng chāyìhuà
A. What is programme differentiation?
Programme differentiation means adjusting teaching, materials, learning activities or expected outcomes to respond to children's different abilities and needs.
课程差异化是指调整教学、材料、学习活动或预期成果，以回应儿童不同的能力和需要。
Kèchéng chāyìhuà shì zhǐ tiáozhěng jiàoxué, cáiliào, xuéxí huódòng huò yùqī chéngguǒ, yǐ huíyìng értóng bùtóng de nénglì hé xūyào.
Differentiation does not mean giving every child a completely separate lesson.
差异化并不意味着为每个儿童安排完全不同的课程。
Chāyìhuà bìng bù yìwèizhe wèi měi gè értóng ānpái wánquán bùtóng de kèchéng.
Children can learn together while receiving different levels or types of support.
儿童可以一起学习，同时获得不同程度或类型的支持。
Értóng kěyǐ yìqǐ xuéxí, tóngshí huòdé bùtóng chéngdù huò lèixíng de zhīchí.
B. Four areas of programme differentiation
1. Content — What children learn
1. 内容——儿童学习什么
Yī. Nèiróng — Értóng xuéxí shénme
Teachers may adjust the amount or complexity of information.
教师可以调整信息的数量或复杂程度。
Jiàoshī kěyǐ tiáozhěng xìnxī de shùliàng huò fùzá chéngdù.
Example: Some children identify two shapes, while others identify four shapes.
例子： 一些儿童识别两种形状，其他儿童识别四种形状。
Lìzi: Yìxiē értóng shíbié liǎng zhǒng xíngzhuàng, qítā értóng shíbié sì zhǒng xíngzhuàng.
2. Process — How children learn
2. 过程——儿童如何学习
Èr. Guòchéng — Értóng rúhé xuéxí
Teachers may use pictures, demonstrations, real objects or additional practice.
教师可以使用图片、示范、实物或额外练习。
Jiàoshī kěyǐ shǐyòng túpiàn, shìfàn, shíwù huò éwài liànxí.
3. Product — How children show learning
3. 成果——儿童如何展示学习
