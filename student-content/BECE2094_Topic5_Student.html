A. What is a learning disability?
A. 什么是学习障碍？
A. Shénme shì xuéxí zhàng'ài?
A learning disability affects how a person learns or uses particular academic skills, such as reading, writing or mathematics.
学习障碍会影响一个人学习或运用某些学科技能的方式，例如阅读、写作或数学。
Xuéxí zhàng'ài huì yǐngxiǎng yí gè rén xuéxí huò yùnyòng mǒuxiē xuékē jìnéng de fāngshì, lìrú yuèdú, xiězuò huò shùxué.
A child with a specific learning disability may have average or above-average intelligence.
有特定学习障碍的儿童可能具有一般或高于一般的智力。
Yǒu tèdìng xuéxí zhàng'ài de értóng kěnéng jùyǒu yìbān huò gāo yú yìbān de zhìlì.
Learning disabilities are not caused simply by laziness, poor parenting or a lack of effort.
学习障碍并不只是由懒惰、不良教养或缺乏努力造成的。
Xuéxí zhàng'ài bìng bù zhǐ shì yóu lǎnduò, bùliáng jiàoyǎng huò quēfá nǔlì zàochéng de.
Important distinction
重要区别
Zhòngyào qūbié
In this topic, we focus mainly on specific learning disabilities involving academic skills.
在本主题中，我们主要关注影响学科技能的特定学习障碍。
Zài běn zhǔtí zhōng, wǒmen zhǔyào guānzhù yǐngxiǎng xuékē jìnéng de tèdìng xuéxí zhàng'ài.
The term learning difficulties is broader and may describe problems caused by language differences, limited learning opportunities, sensory needs or other factors.
“学习困难”是一个范围更广的术语，可能描述由语言差异、学习机会有限、感官需要或其他因素造成的问题。
“Xuéxí kùnnan” shì yí gè fànwéi gèng guǎng de shùyǔ, kěnéng miáoshù yóu yǔyán chāyì, xuéxí jīhuì yǒuxiàn, gǎnguān xūyào huò qítā yīnsù zàochéng de wèntí.

3. 数学学习障碍——数学方面的困难
Sān. Shùxué xuéxí zhàng'ài — Shùxué fāngmiàn de kùnnan

数学学习障碍影响数字或数学概念的学习和理解。

A child may find counting, comparing quantities or remembering number facts difficult.

C. Early childhood considerations
C. 幼儿期相关考量
C. Yòu'ér qī xiāngguān kǎoliáng
Specific learning disabilities are often more clearly identified after children begin formal reading, writing or mathematics instruction.
特定学习障碍通常在儿童开始接受正式的阅读、写作或数学教学后更容易被识别。
Tèdìng xuéxí zhàng'ài tōngcháng zài értóng kāishǐ jiēshòu zhèngshì de yuèdú, xiězuò huò shùxué jiàoxué hòu gèng róngyì bèi shíbié.
Preschool teachers may notice early warning signs, but these signs alone do not confirm a disability.
幼儿教师可能注意到早期警示迹象，但这些迹象本身并不能确定儿童有学习障碍。
Yòu'ér jiàoshī kěnéng zhùyì dào zǎoqī jǐngshì jìxiàng, dàn zhèxiē jìxiàng běnshēn bìng bù néng quèdìng értóng yǒu xuéxí zhàng'ài.
For example, a child may find rhyming, recognising letters, counting objects or remembering simple sequences difficult.
例如，儿童可能在押韵、识别字母、数物品或记住简单顺序方面遇到困难。
Lìrú, értóng kěnéng zài yāyùn, shíbié zìmǔ, shǔ wùpǐn huò jìzhù jiǎndān shùnxù fāngmiàn yùdào kùnnan.
Teachers should consider the child's age, language exposure, learning opportunities and developmental history.
教师应该考虑儿童的年龄、语言接触情况、学习机会和发展历史。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi kǎolǜ értóng de niánlíng, yǔyán jiēchù qíngkuàng, xuéxí jīhuì hé fāzhǎn lìshǐ.
D. Five educational considerations
D. 五项教育考量
D. Wǔ xiàng jiàoyù kǎoliáng
1. Recognise individual strengths
1. 认识个人优势
Yī. Rènshi gèrén yōushì
A child who finds reading difficult may be creative, curious or skilled in practical activities.
阅读有困难的儿童可能富有创造力、好奇心，或擅长实际操作活动。
Yuèdú yǒu kùnnan de értóng kěnéng fùyǒu chuàngzàolì, hàoqíxīn, huò shàncháng shíjì cāozuò huódòng.
2. Avoid negative labels
2. 避免负面标签
Èr. Bìmiǎn fùmiàn biāoqiān
Teachers should not describe children as lazy, careless or unintelligent because they struggle with learning.
教师不应该因为儿童学习困难，就说他们懒惰、粗心或不聪明。
Jiàoshī bù yīnggāi yīnwèi értóng xuéxí kùnnan, jiù shuō tāmen lǎnduò, cūxīn huò bù cōngmíng.
3. Provide sufficient practice
3. 提供足够练习
Sān. Tígōng zúgòu liànxí
Some children benefit from repeated, structured practice using different senses.
一些儿童可以通过重复、有结构及运用不同感官的练习获得帮助。
Yìxiē értóng kěyǐ tōngguò chóngfù, yǒu jiégòu jí yùnyòng bùtóng gǎnguān de liànxí huòdé bāngzhù.
4. Use suitable assessment methods
4. 使用适当的评估方法
Sì. Shǐyòng shìdàng de pínggū fāngfǎ
Allow children to demonstrate understanding through speech, pointing, matching or practical activities.
让儿童通过说话、指认、配对或实际操作活动展示理解。
Ràng értóng tōngguò shuōhuà, zhǐrèn, pèiduì huò shíjì cāozuò huódòng zhǎnshì lǐjiě.
5. Work with families and specialists
5. 与家庭和专业人员合作
Wǔ. Yǔ jiātíng hé zhuānyè rényuán hézuò
Share factual observations and discuss suitable support without making an unsupported diagnosis.
分享客观观察结果，并讨论适当的支持方法，而不作出没有依据的诊断。
Fēnxiǎng kèguān guānchá jiéguǒ, bìng tǎolùn shìdàng de zhīchí fāngfǎ, ér bù zuòchū méiyǒu yījù de zhěnduàn.

5.2 Early Intervention
5.2 早期干预
5.2 Zǎoqī gānyù
A. What is early intervention for learning difficulties?
A. 什么是针对学习困难的早期干预？
A. Shénme shì zhēnduì xuéxí kùnnán de zǎoqí gānyù?
Early intervention means identifying learning needs early and providing suitable support before difficulties become more serious.
早期干预是指及早识别学习需要，并在困难变得更严重之前提供适当支持。
Zǎoqī gānyù shì zhǐ jízǎo shíbié xuéxí xūyào, bìng zài kùnnan biàn de gèng yánzhòng zhīqián tígōng shìdàng zhīchí.
For young children, early intervention should be developmentally appropriate, supportive and often play-based.
对于幼儿，早期干预应该符合发展阶段、具有支持性，并经常以游戏为基础。
Duìyú yòu'ér, zǎoqī gānyù yīnggāi fúhé fāzhǎn jiēduàn, jùyǒu zhīchíxìng, bìng jīngcháng yǐ yóuxì wéi jīchǔ.
Children do not need a confirmed learning disability diagnosis to receive appropriate classroom teaching support.
儿童不需要先获得确定的学习障碍诊断，才能接受适当的课堂教学支持。
Értóng bù xūyào xiān huòdé quèdìng de xuéxí zhàng'ài zhěnduàn, cái néng jiēshòu shìdàng de kètáng jiàoxué zhīchí.

Shǐyòng shìdàng de yāyùn, yīnjié hé yǔyīn yìshí yóuxì, bāngzhù értóng zhùyì kǒuyǔ zhōng de shēngyīn.
3. Early number sense
3. 早期数感
Sān. Zǎoqī shùgǎn
Use real objects to practise counting, matching and comparing quantities.
使用实物来练习数数、配对和比较数量。
Shǐyòng shíwù lái liànxí shǔshù, pèiduì hé bǐjiào shùliàng.
4. Fine motor and writing readiness
4. 精细动作和书写准备能力
Sì. Jīngxì dòngzuò hé shūxiě zhǔnbèi nénglì
Provide drawing, playdough, threading and other suitable hand-control activities.
提供画画、玩黏土、穿珠和其他适当的手部控制活动。
Tígōng huàhuà, wán niántǔ, chuānzhū hé qítā shìdàng de shǒubù kòngzhì huódòng.
5. Attention and working memory
5. 注意力和工作记忆
Wǔ. Zhùyìlì hé gōngzuò jìyì
Use short instructions, predictable routines and simple memory games.
使用简短指示、可预测的日常程序和简单记忆游戏。
Shǐyòng jiǎnduǎn zhǐshì, kě yùcè de rìcháng chéngxù hé jiǎndān jìyì yóuxì.

D. Example of a measurable early intervention goal
D. 可衡量的早期干预目标示例
D. Kě héngliáng de zǎoqí gānyù mùbiāo shìlì
Situation: A five-year-old child has difficulty matching quantities to numerals from 1 to 5.
情境： 一名五岁儿童难以将一至五的数量与数字配对。
Qíngjìng: Yì míng wǔ suì értóng nányǐ jiāng yī zhì wǔ de shùliàng yǔ shùzì pèiduì.
Goal: Within four weeks, the child will match quantities of one to three objects to the correct numeral in four out of five opportunities, using counters and picture support.
目标： 在四周内，儿童将在使用计数物和图片支持的情况下，于五次机会中至少四次正确地将一至三个物品的数量与相应数字配对。
Mùbiāo: Zài sì zhōu nèi, értóng jiāng zài shǐyòng jìshùwù hé túpiàn zhīchí de qíngkuàng xià, yú wǔ cì jīhuì zhōng zhìshǎo sì cì zhèngquè de jiāng yī zhì sān gè wùpǐn de shùliàng yǔ xiāngyìng shùzì pèiduì.
The teacher records performance during short, enjoyable counting games.
教师在简短而有趣的数数游戏中记录儿童的表现。
Jiàoshī zài jiǎnduǎn ér yǒuqù de shǔshù yóuxì zhōng jìlù értóng de biǎoxiàn.
