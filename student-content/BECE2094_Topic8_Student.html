8.1 Educational Consideration
8.1 教育方面的考量
8.1 Jiàoyù fāngmiàn de kǎoliáng
A. What are Emotional or Behavioural Disorders?
A. 什么是情绪或行为障碍？
A. Shénme shì qíngxù huò xíngwéi zhàng’ài?
Emotional or Behavioural Disorders (EBD) is an educational term used to describe significant emotional or behavioural difficulties that may interfere with a child's learning, relationships or participation.
情绪或行为障碍（EBD）是一个教育术语，用来描述可能影响儿童学习、人际关系或参与活动的明显情绪或行为困难。
Qíngxù huò xíngwéi zhàng'ài (EBD) shì yí gè jiàoyù shùyǔ, yòng lái miáoshù kěnéng yǐngxiǎng értóng xuéxí, rénjì guānxì huò cānyù huódòng de míngxiǎn qíngxù huò xíngwéi kùnnan.
The term does not refer to one single medical diagnosis. Definitions and eligibility criteria may differ between education systems.
这个术语并不代表单一的医学诊断。不同教育制度的定义和资格标准可能有所不同。
Zhège shùyǔ bìng bù dàibiǎo dānyī de yīxué zhěnduàn. Bùtóng jiàoyù zhìdù de dìngyì hé zīgé biāozhǔn kěnéng yǒu suǒ bùtóng.
Young children may sometimes cry, refuse activities, become angry or have difficulty managing their emotions.
幼儿有时可能哭泣、拒绝活动、生气或难以管理自己的情绪。
Yòu'ér yǒushí kěnéng kūqì, jùjué huódòng, shēngqì huò nányǐ guǎnlǐ zìjǐ de qíngxù.
These behaviours alone do not mean that a child has EBD.
这些行为本身并不代表儿童有EBD。
Zhèxiē xíngwéi běnshēn bìng bù dàibiǎo értóng yǒu EBD.
Teachers should consider the child's age, development, environment, communication abilities and the frequency, duration and impact of the difficulties.
教师应该考虑儿童的年龄、发展、环境、沟通能力，以及困难出现的频率、持续时间和影响。
Jiàoshī yīnggāi kǎolǜ értóng de niánlíng, fāzhǎn, huánjìng, gōutōng nénglì, yǐjí kùnnan chūxiàn de pínlǜ, chíxù shíjiān hé yǐngxiǎng.
B. Common types of emotional or behavioural difficulties
B. 常见的情绪或行为困难类型
B. Chángjiàn de qíngxù huò xíngwéi kùnnan lèixíng
1. Externalising behaviours
1. 外显行为
Yī. Wàixiǎn xíngwéi
These are behaviours that are more visible to other people.
这些行为比较容易被其他人看见。
Zhèxiē xíngwéi bǐjiào róngyì bèi qítā rén kànjiàn.
Examples include frequent shouting, hitting, throwing objects or strongly resisting classroom activities.
例子包括经常大喊、打人、扔东西或强烈抗拒课堂活动。
Lìzi bāokuò jīngcháng dàhǎn, dǎ rén, rēng dōngxi huò qiángliè kàngjù kètáng huódòng.
2. Internalising difficulties
2. 内隐性困难
Èr. Nèiyǐnxìng kùnnan
These difficulties may be less noticeable because the child expresses distress inwardly.
这些困难可能不太明显，因为儿童将痛苦表现在内心。
Zhèxiē kùnnan kěnéng bú tài míngxiǎn, yīnwèi értóng jiāng tòngkǔ biǎoxiàn zài nèixīn.
Examples include persistent anxiety, sadness, withdrawal or avoiding social activities.
例子包括持续焦虑、悲伤、退缩或回避社交活动。
Lìzi bāokuò chíxù jiāolǜ, bēishāng, tuìsuō huò huíbì shèjiāo huódòng.
3. Difficulties with emotional regulation
3. 情绪调节困难
Sān. Qíngxù tiáojié kùnnan
Some children need additional support to recognise emotions, recover from frustration or cope with unexpected changes.
一些儿童需要额外支持，才能识别情绪、从挫折中恢复或应对突然的变化。
Yìxiē értóng xūyào éwài zhīchí, cái néng shíbié qíngxù, cóng cuòzhé zhōng huīfù huò yìngduì tūrán de biànhuà.
4. Difficulties with relationships
4. 人际关系困难
Sì. Rénjì guānxì kùnnan
Some children may find it difficult to build friendships, trust adults or manage disagreements.
一些儿童可能难以建立友谊、信任成人或处理分歧。
Yìxiē értóng kěnéng nányǐ jiànlì yǒuyì, xìnrèn chéngrén huò chǔlǐ fēnqí.
C. Factors that may influence behaviour
C. 可能影响行为的因素
C. Kěnéng yǐngxiǎng xíngwéi de yīnsù
Children's emotional and behavioural difficulties can have several interacting causes.
儿童的情绪和行为困难可能由多个相互影响的因素造成。
Értóng de qíngxù hé xíngwéi kùnnan kěnéng yóu duō gè xiānghù yǐngxiǎng de yīnsù zàochéng.
These may include developmental differences, communication difficulties, sleep problems, stress, sensory discomfort, changes in routines or difficult life experiences.
这些因素可能包括发展差异、沟通困难、睡眠问题、压力、感官不适、日常程序变化或困难的生活经历。
Zhèxiē yīnsù kěnéng bāokuò fāzhǎn chāyì, gōutōng kùnnan, shuìmián wèntí, yālì, gǎnguān bùshì, rìcháng chéngxù biànhuà huò kùnnan de shēnghuó jīnglì.
Teachers should not assume that challenging behaviour always means deliberate disobedience.
教师不应该假设具有挑战性的行为总是故意不服从。
Jiàoshī bù yīnggāi jiǎshè jùyǒu tiǎozhànxìng de xíngwéi zǒng shì gùyì bù fúcóng.
D. Important educational considerations
D. 重要的教育考量
D. Zhòngyào de jiàoyù kǎoliáng
1. Build trusting relationships
1. 建立信任关系
Yī. Jiànlì xìnrèn guānxì
Use a calm voice, listen to the child and respond consistently.
使用平静的语气、倾听儿童，并作出一致的回应。
Shǐyòng píngjìng de yǔqì, qīngtīng értóng, bìng zuòchū yízhì de huíyìng.
2. Observe behaviour objectively
2. 客观观察行为
Èr. Kèguān guānchá xíngwéi
Record what happened before, during and after the behaviour instead of describing the child with labels such as “naughty”.
记录行为发生之前、期间和之后的情况，而不是使用“顽皮”等标签描述儿童。
Jìlù xíngwéi fāshēng zhīqián, qījiān hé zhīhòu de qíngkuàng, ér bú shì shǐyòng “wánpí” děng biāoqiān miáoshù értóng.
3. Provide predictable routines
3. 提供可预测的日常程序
Sān. Tígōng kě yùcè de rìcháng chéngxù
Use clear expectations, visual reminders and advance warnings of changes.
使用清楚的行为期望、视觉提醒和提前通知变化。
Shǐyòng qīngchǔ de xíngwéi qīwàng, shìjué tíxǐng hé tíqián tōngzhī biànhuà.
4. Teach emotional skills
4. 教授情绪技能
Sì. Jiàoshòu qíngxù jìnéng
Help children identify feelings, ask for support and practise calming strategies.
帮助儿童识别感受、寻求支持并练习平静策略。
Bāngzhù értóng shíbié gǎnshòu, xúnqiú zhīchí bìng liànxí píngjìng cèlüè.
5. Protect dignity and inclusion
5. 保护尊严和融合参与
Wǔ. Bǎohù zūnyán hé rónghé cānyù
Avoid public humiliation, harsh punishment and unnecessary exclusion from learning.
避免公开羞辱、严厉惩罚和不必要地排除儿童参与学习。
Bìmiǎn gōngkāi xiūrǔ, yánlì chéngfá hé bù bìyào de páichú értóng cānyù xuéxí.
6. Work with families and professionals
6. 与家庭和专业人员合作
Liù. Yǔ jiātíng hé zhuānyè rényuán hézuò
Share observations respectfully and seek professional assessment or support when difficulties are persistent or serious.
以尊重的方式分享观察记录，并在困难持续或严重时寻求专业评估或支持。
Yǐ zūnzhòng de fāngshì fēnxiǎng guānchá jìlù, bìng zài kùnnan chíxù huò yánzhòng shí xúnqiú zhuānyè pínggū huò zhīchí.
Teachers support children; they do not diagnose mental health or behavioural disorders.
教师负责支持儿童，而不是诊断心理健康或行为障碍。
Jiàoshī fùzé zhīchí értóng, ér bú shì zhěnduàn xīnlǐ jiànkāng huò xíngwéi zhàng'ài.

E. 学前教育实例
E. Xuéqián jiàoyù shílì
Malaysia — A child who frequently shouts
马来西亚——经常大喊的儿童

一名五岁儿童在被要求停止游戏时经常大喊。

中国——回避小组活动的儿童
Zhōngguó — Huíbì xiǎozǔ huódòng de értóng

一名幼儿园儿童经常独处，并在小组游戏中显得担忧。
Yì míng yòu'éryuán értóng jīngcháng dúchǔ, bìng zài xiǎozǔ yóuxì zhōng xiǎnde dānyōu.
The teacher builds trust, offers a choice of activities and gradually supports participation without pressure.
教师建立信任、提供活动选择，并在没有压力的情况下逐渐支持参与。
Jiàoshī jiànlì xìnrèn, tígōng huódòng xuǎnzé, bìng zài méiyǒu yālì de qíngkuàng xià zhújiàn zhīchí cānyù.

8.2 Early Intervention
8.2 早期干预
8.2 Zǎoqī gānyù
A. What is early intervention?
A. 什么是早期干预？
A. Shénme shì zǎoqī gānyù?
Early intervention means providing timely, individualised support before emotional or behavioural difficulties become more serious.
早期干预是指及时提供个别化支持，避免情绪或行为困难变得更加严重。
Zǎoqī gānyù shì zhǐ jíshí tígōng gèbiéhuà zhīchí, bìmiǎn qíngxù huò xíngwéi kùnnan biàn de gèngjiā yánzhòng.
Support should focus on understanding the child's needs, teaching useful skills and improving the environment.
支持应该着重了解儿童的需要、教授实用技能和改善环境。
Zhīchí yīnggāi zhuózhòng liǎojiě értóng de xūyào, jiàoshòu shíyòng jìnéng hé gǎishàn huánjìng.
Children do not need to wait for a formal diagnosis before receiving appropriate classroom support.
儿童不需要等待正式诊断后才获得适当的课堂支持。
Értóng bù xūyào děngdài zhèngshì zhěnduàn hòu cái huòdé shìdàng de kètáng zhīchí.
B. Five important early intervention approaches
B. 五种重要的早期干预方法
B. Wǔ zhǒng zhòngyào de zǎoqī gānyù fāngfǎ
1. Positive Behaviour Support (PBS)
1. 积极行为支持（PBS）
Yī. Jījí xíngwéi zhīchí (PBS)
Positive Behaviour Support aims to understand why behaviour occurs, prevent unnecessary difficulties and teach useful alternative behaviours.
积极行为支持旨在了解行为发生的原因、预防不必要的困难，并教授有用的替代行为。
Jījí xíngwéi zhīchí zhǐzài liǎojiě xíngwéi fāshēng de yuányīn, yùfáng bù bìyào de kùnnan, bìng jiàoshòu yǒuyòng de tìdài xíngwéi.
For example, a child who pushes materials away may learn to request help or a short break.
例如，一名推开材料的儿童可以学习寻求帮助或请求短暂休息。
Lìrú, yì míng tuīkāi cáiliào de értóng kěyǐ xuéxí xúnqiú bāngzhù huò qǐngqiú duǎnzàn xiūxi.
2. Social-emotional learning
2. 社会情绪学习
Èr. Shèhuì qíngxù xuéxí
Teach children to recognise feelings, understand others, solve simple problems and develop relationships.
教导儿童识别感受、理解他人、解决简单问题和发展人际关系。
Jiàodǎo értóng shíbié gǎnshòu, lǐjiě tārén, jiějué jiǎndān wèntí hé fāzhǎn rénjì guānxì.
3. Co-regulation
3. 共同情绪调节
Sān. Gòngtóng qíngxù tiáojié
Co-regulation occurs when a supportive adult helps a child manage strong emotions through a calm presence and appropriate assistance.
共同情绪调节是指支持性的成人通过平静的陪伴和适当协助，帮助儿童管理强烈情绪。
Gòngtóng qíngxù tiáojié shì zhǐ zhīchíxìng de chéngrén tōngguò píngjìng de péibàn hé shìdàng xiézhù, bāngzhù értóng guǎnlǐ qiángliè qíngxù.
Children gradually develop independent regulation skills through repeated supportive experiences.
儿童通过反复获得支持性的经验，逐渐发展独立调节情绪的技能。
Értóng tōngguò fǎnfù huòdé zhīchíxìng de jīngyàn, zhújiàn fāzhǎn dúlì tiáojié qíngxù de jìnéng.
4. Family collaboration
4. 家庭合作
Sì. Jiātíng hézuò
Teachers and caregivers can share observations, identify useful strategies and practise consistent support.
教师和照顾者可以分享观察记录、识别有用策略，并练习一致的支持方法。
Jiàoshī hé zhàogùzhě kěyǐ fēnxiǎng guānchá jìlù, shíbié yǒuyòng cèlüè, bìng liànxí yízhì de zhīchí fāngfǎ.
Family involvement should be respectful and should not assume that parents caused the difficulties.
家庭参与应该建立在尊重的基础上，不应该假设父母造成了这些困难。
Jiātíng cānyù yīnggāi jiànlì zài zūnzhòng de jīchǔ shàng, bù yīnggāi jiǎshè fùmǔ zàochéng le zhèxiē kùnnan.
5. Professional collaboration
5. 专业人员合作
Wǔ. Zhuānyè rényuán hézuò
When needed, teachers may collaborate with appropriately qualified psychologists, counsellors, healthcare professionals or other specialists.
在需要时，教师可以与具备适当资格的心理学家、辅导员、医疗专业人员或其他专家合作。
Zài xūyào shí, jiàoshī kěyǐ yǔ jùbèi shìdàng zīgé de xīnlǐxuéjiā, fǔdǎoyuán, yīliáo zhuānyè rényuán huò qítā zhuānjiā hézuò.

D. Planning a measurable intervention goal
D. 制定可衡量的干预目标
D. Zhìdìng kě héngliáng de gānyù mùbiāo
Situation
情境
Qíngjìng
A child often throws crayons when a drawing activity becomes difficult.
一名儿童在绘画活动变得困难时经常扔蜡笔。

8.4 Time-Out
8.4 暂时离开强化情境
8.4 Zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng
A. What is time-out?
A. 什么是暂时离开强化情境？
A. Shénme shì zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng?
In behaviour management, time-out from positive reinforcement is a procedure involving a brief removal of access to reinforcing activities or attention following a specified behaviour.
在行为管理中，暂时离开正强化情境是一种程序，指在特定行为发生后，短暂减少儿童接触具有强化作用的活动或关注的机会。
Zài xíngwéi guǎnlǐ zhōng, zànshí líkāi zhèng qiánghuà qíngjìng shì yì zhǒng chéngxù, zhǐ zài tèdìng xíngwéi fāshēng hòu, duǎnzàn jiǎnshǎo értóng jiēchù jùyǒu qiánghuà zuòyòng de huódòng huò guānzhù de jīhuì.
Time-out is different from a child voluntarily using a quiet space to regulate emotions.
暂时离开强化情境与儿童自愿使用安静空间来调节情绪不同。
Zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng yǔ értóng zìyuàn shǐyòng ānjìng kōngjiān lái tiáojié qíngxù bùtóng.
Time-out is not a treatment for EBD and should not replace positive behaviour support, relationship-building or teaching replacement skills.
暂时离开强化情境不是治疗EBD的方法，也不应该取代积极行为支持、建立关系或教授替代技能。
Zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng bú shì zhìliáo EBD de fāngfǎ, yě bù yīnggāi qǔdài jījí xíngwéi zhīchí, jiànlì guānxì huò jiàoshòu tìdài jìnéng.

暂时离开强化情境着重行为后果，并不一定能够帮助情绪不安的儿童恢复平静。
Zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng zhuózhòng xíngwéi hòuguǒ, bìng bù yídìng nénggòu bāngzhù qíngxù bù'ān de értóng huīfù píngjìng.

平静空间提供支持性的地方，让儿童在适当的成人协助下调节情绪。
Píngjìng kōngjiān tígōng zhīchíxìng de dìfang, ràng értóng zài shìdàng de chéngrén xiézhù xià tiáojié qíngxù.

Chéngfáxìng gélí kěnéng shǐ értóng hàipà, xiūkuì huò bèi páichì, bù yīnggāi zuòwéi rìcháng jiàoxué cèlüè.
C. Why teachers must be careful
C. 为什么教师必须谨慎
C. Wèishénme jiàoshī bìxū jǐnshèn
Time-out may be inappropriate when behaviour communicates fear, anxiety, sensory overload, unmet needs or difficulty understanding instructions.
当行为表达恐惧、焦虑、感官负荷过重、未被满足的需要或理解指示的困难时，暂时离开强化情境可能不合适。
Dāng xíngwéi biǎodá kǒngjù, jiāolǜ, gǎnguān fùhè guò zhòng, wèi bèi mǎnzú de xūyào huò lǐjiě zhǐshì de kùnnan shí, zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng kěnéng bù héshì.
It may also be ineffective if leaving an activity is exactly what the child needs or is trying to communicate.
如果离开活动正是儿童需要或试图表达的事情，暂时离开强化情境也可能无效。
Rúguǒ líkāi huódòng zhèng shì értóng xūyào huò shìtú biǎodá de shìqing, zànshí líkāi qiánghuà qíngjìng yě kěnéng wúxiào.
For example, removing a child from a difficult task may unintentionally increase the likelihood of the same behaviour occurring again.
例如，让儿童离开困难任务，可能无意中增加相同行为再次发生的可能性。

Children's emotional and behavioural difficulties should be understood in relation to their development, needs and environment.
